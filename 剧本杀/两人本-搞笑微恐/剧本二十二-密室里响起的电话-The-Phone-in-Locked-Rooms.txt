═══════════════════════════════════════════════════
   THE PHONE THAT RINGS IN LOCKED ROOMS 📞
   (A 2-Player Spooky-Comedy Mystery Script)
   
   密室里响起的电话 —— 英文两人微恐+搞笑剧本杀
   Spooky level: 🌑🌑🌑🌑 (genuinely creepy,
   absurdly wholesome truth)
   Age: 10+ | Players: 2 | Time: 40-60 min
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

📖 BACKGROUND (Read Aloud Together)

The student council office has a landline
phone. Old style. Cream colored. It has
been unplugged for THREE YEARS — the
cable hangs loose behind the desk. Everyone
knows this. It's a decoration. A relic.

For six days, it has been RINGING. 📞

Here's the impossible part:

The office is LOCKED every night at 5 pm
by the secretary, Ms. Bright, who has
done it the same way for 12 years (key,
turn, tug the handle twice, say "good-
night, chaos," leave). Every morning,
the office is still locked. Windows
locked. No other entrance. And every
morning this week, students arriving
early report the SAME thing:

At exactly 7:02 am, through the locked
door, they hear the old phone RINGING.
Three rings. Then it stops.

But wait — it gets worse.

Wednesday morning, the janitor unlocked
the door at 7:03 am, one minute AFTER
the rings. He picked up the phone. He
listened. He went completely white, put
it down gently, and told the headteacher,
quote:

  "Someone answered. A voice said,
   'Is it Thursday yet?' and then
   it laughed and hung up."

Today is Thursday. 😨

The whole school is spiraling. The
student council has moved their meetings
to the cafeteria. The office sits empty,
locked, its dead phone preparing to ring
at 7:02 am tomorrow like it has all week.

You two are the school's official
Investigators of Weird Stuff (you founded
it Tuesday, membership: two). You have
until tomorrow 7:02 am. One of you will
be inside the office when it rings.

Night night. 📞🌙

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🎮 HOW TO PLAY

1. Each player secretly reads ONLY their
   own character card. NO PEEKING! 🙈
2. Play 3 rounds (follow the Round Cards).
3. You may LIE about anything EXCEPT the
   two things marked 【CANNOT LIE】.
4. Final round: accuse each other!
5. Then read THE TRUTH together. 🎉

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🅰️ CHARACTER CARD A — JUNO (The Rational One, Currently Struggling)

You are JUNO. You believe in logic,
checklists, and that everything has an
explanation. You started this club to
DEBUNK weird stuff. This week has been
personally difficult for you.

You've mapped the office: one door,
three windows (locked from inside, no
breaks), no vents big enough for a
person, no crawlspaces, no secret
passages (you knocked on every wall —
the caretaker watched you do it and
slowly shook his head the entire time).

The phone is UNPLUGGED. You've checked
FIVE times. You photographed the cable.
The phone should not ring. PHONES NEED
ELECTRICITY. THIS IS BASIC.

YOUR SECRET 🤫:
Yesterday at 6:45 am — before the
ringing — you were in the hallway
outside the office (you'd arrived early
for your "phone surveillance"). At 6:45,
you heard something that broke your
brain a little:

From INSIDE the locked office... a
VOICE. Not the phone. A voice, humming.
A specific song: the school anthem,
hummed slowly, slightly wrong — the
same wrong note in the same place, over
and over. Then, at 7:02, the phone rang
(as always), three rings, silence.

You've told NO ONE about the humming.
Because the alternative to "ghost" is
that a PERSON is inside that office
every morning at 6:45, somehow, humming
the school anthem badly, and then making
an unplugged phone ring, and both
options violate everything you stand
for.

Your notebook now has a page titled
"THINGS I REFUSE TO BELIEVE" and it has
seven items on it. You look at it
nightly.

 Things I Refuse To Believe (so far):
 1. Ghosts
 2. Unplugged phones ringing
 3. Bad humming in locked rooms
 ...

【CANNOT LIE #1】You heard humming from
inside the locked office at 6:45 am
yesterday.

【CANNOT LIE #2】The phone cable was
definitely unplugged every time you
checked.

YOUR TASKS (1 point each):
□ Keep your sanity (partial credit)
□ Reveal the humming evidence without
  saying the g-word
□ Find out why Theo keeps volunteering
  to be locked in the office overnight
□ Figure out who "Is it Thursday yet"
  is waiting for
□ Bonus +3: figure out the FULL truth

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🅱️ CHARACTER CARD B — THEO (The One With Inappropriate Enthusiasm)

You are THEO. You joined this club
because mysteries are EXCITING and also
your mom said you needed a hobby that
doesn't involve "asking the neighbors
about their delivery schedules."

You have been VOLUNTEERING to be locked
in the office overnight "for science"
since Tuesday. Everyone said no. You've
asked four times. It's become a bit.

YOUR SECRET 🤫:
The reason you're so keen? You already
know something. Something big.

Two weeks ago, you were in the office
after school (council work — you're not
on the council, you just like meetings)
and you found, taped UNDER the desk, a
small device. Plastic box. Battery
powered. Speaker. Timer. You poked it,
it made a loud BEEP, and you panicked,
turned it off, and put it back exactly
as you found it.

It's a PHONE RING SOUND MACHINE. Someone
planted it. The "ringing" isn't the dead
phone — it's a hidden speaker playing a
ring sound. Timed. 7:02 am. You realized
this on day one of the mystery. You've
been sitting on the biggest clue of the
week because:

If you reveal it, everyone will ask HOW
you found it. And how you found it is
that you were snooping in the office
doing "council work" you weren't invited
to do. Twice. Okay, three times. You've
been attending student council meetings
uninvited for a MONTH. You have opinions
about the bake sale budget. Nobody asked.

Also — the deeper you've thought about
it — the machine doesn't explain the
JANITOR's story. A machine can't ANSWER
a call and ask "Is it Thursday yet?"
A machine can't hum the school anthem.
So either the machine is a decoy... or
there are TWO mysteries. You've made a
whiteboard at home. It's a lot.

【CANNOT LIE #1】You found a battery-
powered sound device taped under the
desk two weeks ago.

【CANNOT LIE #2】You have attended
student council meetings uninvited for
a month.

YOUR TASKS (1 point each):
□ Reveal the sound machine at the right
  moment (dramatic timing = +1 style)
□ Hide the uninvited meetings situation
□ Find out what Juno heard at 6:45 am
□ Figure out who built the machine and
  WHY THURSDAY
□ Bonus +3: figure out the FULL truth

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🔁 ROUND 1 — THE INTERROGATION (10 min)

Ask each other:
1. Phone's unplugged. Has been for three
   years. Explain. Take your time. We
   have whiteboards.
2. Theo, why do you KEEP volunteering
   to be locked in rooms? Genuinely.
3. Juno, you arrived at 6:45 am yesterday.
   What did you hear? We can do this
   the easy way or the spooky way. 👻

💡 CLUE CARD 1 (read aloud):
"The device under the desk (once you two
finally compare notes and find it): a
cheap 'prank sound machine' — the kind
sold at joke shops. Battery powered.
Programmable. Current program: ring
three times at 7:02 am, daily. It has
been running for six days on a timer.
There's a receipt taped to the back,
from 'Whoopee Emporium,' dated three
weeks ago. The buyer paid cash. The
receipt also includes: '1x rubber
chicken (returns not accepted).' 🐔"

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🔁 ROUND 2 — THE EVIDENCE (10 min)

💡 CLUE CARD 2 (read aloud):
"But the machine doesn't explain the
janitor's call. A sound machine can't
answer questions. Re-interviewed under
calmer conditions, the janitor adds
details: the voice on the phone was
'young. polite. familiar somehow. like
a kid who reads a lot.' And the laugh
wasn't evil. It was... embarrassed?

New fact: the phone's cable, though
unplugged from the WALL, was found
plugged into the PHONE. Both ends
checked. Someone re-connected one end.
The phone itself works. It's an old
landline. It needs the wall connection
to receive calls. It was NOT connected
to the wall this morning. But at 7:02,
with the door locked and cable loose...
it rang ANYWAY. 😨"

💡 CLUE CARD 3 (read aloud):
"Cross-reference: the school's only
other working landline — in the
SECRETARY'S OFFICE NEXT DOOR — has a
call log. At 7:02 am, every morning
this week, ONE internal call was placed:
from the secretary's desk... to the
student council office. Every morning.
Internal calls between the two offices
ring on the old system — the council
office phone receives, even partially
connected, through the building's
ancient internal wiring that maintenance
swears was decommissioned in 2019.

Someone is at the secretary's desk at
7:02 am every morning, calling the
council office, three rings, hanging
up. The secretary, Ms. Bright, arrives
at 7:30. Who is at her desk at 7:02?!"

Ask each other:
1. WHO is in the secretary's office at
   7:02 am every morning?
2. And what does "Is it Thursday yet"
   MEAN?!
3. Also, Theo — uninvited council
   meetings. A month. Explain yourself.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🔁 ROUND 3 — FINAL ACCUSATION (5 min)

Each player declares:
"I accuse you of haunting the phone
because [reasons]! And I ALSO think you
secretly [guess their secret]!"

Reveal:
- Guessed each other's secrets? (+2 each)
- Figured out the FULL truth (the caller,
  the machine, the Thursday question)? (+3)

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🎉 THE TRUTH (Read Aloud Together)

1. There were TWO unexplained things, and
   neither was a ghost. Classic.

2. MYSTERY ONE — The ringing: the prank
   sound machine, planted by... the
   student council PRESIDENT, Priya.
   Why? REVENGE PLOT? No. Schedule
   conflict.

   The council meets Thursdays. Last
   month, attendance was terrible —
   everyone "forgot." So Priya bought a
   prank machine, programmed 7:02 am
   rings in the council office all week,
   and spread the rumor that "the phone
   only stops ringing if the whole
   council shows up Thursday." It was
   a MEETING ATTENDANCE SCHEME. She
   planned to reveal it at Thursday's
   meeting: "The ghost was you not
   coming to meetings. Shame. See you
   all next Thursday."

   The rubber chicken was for a different
   agenda item. The council has moved
   past it. We don't discuss the chicken.

3. MYSTERY TWO — The voice and the
   humming: ORI. A Year 5 kid. Quiet.
   Reads constantly (janitor's
   description: "a kid who reads a lot"
   — accurate). Ori's mom is the school
   secretary's assistant, and Ori comes
   to school with her at 6:40 am every
   day (home is cold, school is warm,
   mom's office has a heater and
   biscuits).

   Ori has been waiting ALL WEEK for
   Thursday — not for a meeting, but
   because Thursday is when the school's
   BOOK DELIVERY arrives. Ori pre-ordered
   the final book of the "Chronicles of
   the Feather King" series EIGHT MONTHS
   ago. It arrives Thursday. Ori has
   been calling the council office phone
   from mom's desk at 7:02 — because the
   plan, hatched with Priya (they're
   cousins; the school didn't know), was
   that Ori would ring three times to
   check the line worked, then at 7:30
   tell Ms. Bright the phone "might be
   haunted, someone should investigate
   the book delivery shelf." Layers.
   Children's schemes have LAYERS.

   The janitor catching the "Is it
   Thursday yet?" call was NOT in the
   plan. Ori panicked-laughed and hung
   up. Hence "embarrassed laugh." Ori
   has felt guilty for three days.

   The humming? Ori, waiting at 6:45,
   headphones on, humming the school
   anthem (it plays in the audiobook
   Ori's been re-reading — the Feather
   King attends school in book three).
   Same wrong note every time because
   the audiobook narrator sings it
   wrong. Ori hums it EXACTLY as wrong
   as the audiobook. Faithful to the
   source material.

4. Tomorrow, 7:02 am, you two were
   waiting in the office. The phone
   rang. You answered on ring one.
   Silence. Then a small voice: "...is
   it Thursday yet?"

   You said: "It's Thursday."

   The voice GASPED. genuinely, joyful,
   book-delivery gasped. Then footsteps
   running, and Ori burst through the
   door (Ms. Bright, now informed,
   unlocked it at 7:00) holding the
   FATHER OF FEATHERS like a trophy.

   Behind Ori: Priya, holding a rubber
   chicken, looking deeply satisfied
   with her meeting attendance numbers
   (perfect, for the first time all
   term).

5. The student council voted the phone
   "officially haunted (affectionate)."
   It rings every Thursday at 7:02 now —
   Ori calls from the secretary's office
   to say "it's book day" and everyone
   in the office answers with the wrong-
   note anthem hum. It's tradition now.
   The sound machine was retired to the
   trophy shelf, next to the chicken.

6. Juno's "Things I Refuse To Believe"
   list now has a note in the margin:
   "Everything on this list happened.
   I'm starting a new list: 'Things
   That Are Weird But Fine.'" It's
   longer. It's better for sleeping.

WINNER: The player with more points!
EVERYONE: wins, because the ghost was a
nine-year-old waiting for a book, and
a president desperate for quorum. 📞📚✨

📚 GLOSSARY 词汇表

- landline = 座机电话
- unplugged = 拔了插头的
- debunk = 揭穿/证伪（Juno的事业）
- sound machine = 发声机器（恶作剧道具 🐔）
- quorum = 法定开会人数（Priya的执念）
- internal call = 内线电话
- schedule conflict = 时间冲突
- faithful to the source material = 忠于原著（连唱错都忠于 😂）
- tradition = 传统（每周四的铃声）
- affectionate (haunted) = 亲昵意义上的闹鬼
