═══════════════════════════════════════════════════
   THE WHISPERS IN THE LIBRARY 📚👻
   (A 2-Player Spooky-Comedy Mystery Script)
   
   图书馆的低语 —— 英文两人微恐+搞笑剧本杀
   Spooky level: 🌑🌑🌑 (buildup is creepy,
   truth is hilarious)
   Age: 10+ | Players: 2 | Time: 40-60 min
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

📖 BACKGROUND (Read Aloud Together)

The old wing of Bramblewood Library is
closed after 4 pm. It has been for years.

Students who forgot their things and went
back in the evening all tell the SAME story:

  "The books WHISPER." 😨

Not words you can hear clearly. A rustle.
A hiss. Like a hundred pages breathing.
One kid swears a voice said her NAME.
Another says the lights flickered three
times — always three times — and then a
SHADOW slid between Shelf 9 and Shelf 10,
where nobody ever walks, because Shelf 10
is the DICTIONARY SHELF and dictionaries
are boring even to ghosts.

Last night, the night guard, Mr. Pinch,
quit. He left a note on the door:

  "I heard it say MY name.
   Clear as day. I'm done.
   Good luck, everyone.
   — Pinch"

The school wants to reopen the old wing
for the Book Fair on Friday. The principal
needs answers by Thursday.

You two are the last students who were in
the old wing yesterday after 4 pm.
The security log says so. Which makes you
witnesses... or suspects. 🕵️

Find out what whispers in the library
before Friday. And maybe sleep with the
lights on until then. 👻

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🎮 HOW TO PLAY

1. Each player secretly reads ONLY their
   own character card. NO PEEKING! 🙈
2. Play 3 rounds (follow the Round Cards).
3. You may LIE about anything EXCEPT the
   two things marked 【CANNOT LIE】.
4. Final round: accuse each other!
5. Then read THE TRUTH together. 🎉

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🅰️ CHARACTER CARD A — WREN (The Bookworm Who Knows Too Much)

You are WREN. You have read every book in
the old wing. Twice. You love that place
more than your own living room (your living
room has "bad vibes and a broken clock").

Yesterday at 4:12 pm, you were STILL in
the old wing (you hid when the closing
announcement came, classic Wren move) —
and you heard it.

THE WHISPER. 😨

And here's the part you haven't told
ANYONE: the whisper said a word. Not a
name. A word. And the word was...

..."sock." 🧦

You are 90% sure it said "sock." But the
library doesn't HAVE socks. Books don't
WEAR socks. You've checked the catalog.
There is no book called Sock. There is no
AUTHOR named Sock. WHY DID THE GHOST SAY
SOCK?

YOUR SECRET 🤫:
You were in the old wing after hours
(illegal!). If anyone finds out you hid
past closing time, you're banned from the
library for a month. A MONTH. You would
rather face the ghost.

ALSO — at 4:30 pm, you heard footsteps
behind Shelf 9, and you bolted, knocking
over the NEW AUDIOBOOK DISPLAY (the
school just installed it last week —
it has MOTION SENSORS and headphones).
You ran out the fire exit and told no one.
You don't know if you broke it. The guilt
keeps you awake. Not as awake as "sock,"
but close.

【CANNOT LIE #1】You were in the old wing
after 4 pm yesterday.

【CANNOT LIE #2】You heard the whisper say
a word, and the word was "sock."

YOUR TASKS (1 point each):
□ Hide that you broke/possibly broke the
  audiobook display
□ Convince Ash you're not the "ghost"
□ Find out why Ash was ALSO in the old
  wing after 4 pm 👀
□ Figure out what "sock" means
□ Bonus +3: figure out the FULL truth

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🅱️ CHARACTER CARD B — ASH (The New Kid With a Big Secret)

You are ASH. You transferred here three
weeks ago. You don't know anyone. You eat
lunch with your tray angled 45 degrees
away from humanity.

You have a SECRET, and it's the whole
reason you're always the last to leave
the library:

YOUR SECRET 🤫:
Your family is LIVING IN THE SCHOOL
PARKING LOT — in your dad's camper van —
for two weeks, while you wait for your new
apartment to be ready. Money is tight.
Your dad works nights. The van is cold.

So every evening, you "accidentally" stay
in the warm library until the very last
minute. Yesterday, you stayed even later
than Wren — you didn't hide, you were just
reading in the corner behind Shelf 10
(the dictionary shelf — warm, because it's
next to the old heater pipe).

You heard the whisper too. You also heard
Wren bolt and crash into something. You
kept quiet (new kid rules: be invisible).

And one more thing. A small thing. Your
SOCK has a hole. A big one. Your toe has
been "ventilated" for a week. Yesterday,
while you were reading, the heater vent
PULLED your loose sock thread and your
sock came HALF OFF, and you took it off
completely to fix it... and then you heard
Wren running, panicked, grabbed everything
and ran... leaving ONE SOCK behind.
Shelf 10. Dictionary shelf. 🧦

You went back for it this morning at 7 am.
IT WAS GONE.

【CANNOT LIE #1】You were in the old wing
past 4 pm yesterday.

【CANNOT LIE #2】You lost a sock in the
library yesterday.

YOUR TASKS (1 point each):
□ Hide the camper van situation (if Wren
  guesses it, +2 to Wren)
□ Find your missing sock (justice for Ash)
□ Figure out what Wren crashed into
□ Discover who/what the whisper really is
□ Bonus +3: figure out the FULL truth

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🔁 ROUND 1 — THE INTERROGATION (10 min)
(Suggest: read this in a slightly spooky
voice. Slowly. 🕯️)

Ask each other:
1. What were you doing in the old wing
   after 4 pm? The TRUTH. 👀
2. Wren: you ran out the fire exit. What
   did you see? Or... who did you see?
3. Ash: why does one of your shoes have a
   sock in it and the other doesn't? 🧦🤨

💡 CLUE CARD 1 (read aloud):
"The librarian, Ms. Quillfeather, checked
the old wing at 7:30 am. On Shelf 10 —
the dictionary shelf — she found:
one (1) child's sock, flattened, warm,
and smelling faintly of lavender soap.
She also reports the heater pipe behind
Shelf 10 'hums a little tune when it
warms up. Like it's singing. I've told
maintenance 4 times. They sent a post-it
that says WHISTLING IS NORMAL."

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🔁 ROUND 2 — THE EVIDENCE (10 min)

💡 CLUE CARD 2 (read aloud):
"Analysis of the 'whisper' — the science
teacher put her phone on record in the old
wing overnight. Result: the 'whisper' is
REAL but it's not words... it's the new
AUDIOBOOK DISPLAY. Its motion sensor is
set to MAXIMUM sensitivity. When the old
wing heats up in the evening, the warm air
moves, the sensor thinks someone is there,
and it starts playing audiobooks on
WHISPER VOLUME through the shelf speakers.

It has been playing, at random, all week:
a biography of a man named SOCKMAN? No —
wait. It's been playing the audiobook of
'CHARLOTTE'S WEB.' The narrator whispers
'salutations' every time the sensor trips.
Multiple times an evening. Into an empty
room. For a week. 😨😂"

💡 CLUE CARD 3 (read aloud):
"Mr. Pinch, the night guard who quit —
update: he has been interviewed. The
'voice saying his name'? The audiobook
after 'Charlotte's Web' on the display
queue is a mystery novel called
'THE PINCH OF THE POCKET THIEF.' The
display whispers 'Pinch... Pinch...
Pinch...' all night. He heard his own
name from an empty library for a WEEK
before quitting. He is doing better now.
He has bought earplugs and a parrot (the
parrot also says Pinch now; he regrets
the parrot)."

Ask each other:
1. So WHO took Ash's sock this morning?!
2. The shadow between Shelf 9 and 10...?
3. And who set the motion sensor to
   MAXIMUM, hmm?? WHO?! 🕵️

══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🔁 ROUND 3 — FINAL ACCUSATION (5 min)

Each player declares:
"I accuse you of being the Library Ghost
because [reasons]! And I ALSO think you
secretly [guess their secret]!"

Reveal:
- Guessed each other's secrets? (+2 each)
- Figured out the FULL truth (sock, sensor,
  shadow, and who set it to max)? (+3)

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🎉 THE TRUTH (Read Aloud Together)

1. The "ghost whisper" = the new audiobook
   display with its motion sensor set to
   MAXIMUM, playing 'Charlotte's Web' and
   'The Pinch of the Pocket Thief' on
   whisper volume into an empty room all
   week. The word Wren heard — "sock" —
   was actually the narrator saying
   "SALUTATIONS." At whisper volume, behind
   a shelf, in the dark, to a frightened
   kid with excellent hearing... "sock."
   Honestly? Understandable. 🕷️

2. The sensor was set to MAXIMUM by —
   WREN. Three weeks ago. During the
   audiobook display's GRAND OPENING, Wren
   (excited, first in line) pressed every
   button on the demo unit "to see what
   they did." One of them was SENSITIVITY.
   The manual says: "Do not set to max.
   It will detect warm air and small moths."
   Wren is the ghost's creator. Wren has
   known this since Clue Card 2 and has
   been sweating through this entire case.

3. The flickering lights: real, but it's
   the old wing's ancient wiring doing
   its nightly "sunset performance."
   Maintenance post-it #5 says
   "FLICKER IS NORMAL." Everything in this
   school is NORMAL, apparently.

4. The shadow between Shelf 9 and 10:
   Ms. Quillfeather's long winter coat,
   hanging on the shelf end where she
   always forgets it. In the dark, with
   the lights doing their "sunset
   performance," the coat's shadow slides
   across the aisle when the heater
   breeze moves it. A coat. Menacingly.
   For years. 🧥

5. And the sock — THE SOCK — Ms.
   Quillfeather found it at 7:30 am,
   recognized it as "a child's sock,
   warm, lavender soap, clearly loved,"
   and PINNED IT TO THE LOST & FOUND
   BOARD with a note: "To the sock's
   owner: the old wing is closed after
   4 pm. Also, I have KNIT YOU A
   REPLACEMENT PAIR. Purple. Warm.
   Come find me. No questions asked."
   Ash collected them at lunch. They fit
   perfectly. Ash did cry a little.
   Nobody saw. (Ms. Quillfeather saw.
   She said nothing. Legend.)

6. On Friday, the Book Fair opened in the
   old wing. The audiobook display was
   fixed — sensor set to LOW, volume set
   to normal — and it became the most
   popular booth: "THE HAUNTED SHELF,"
   where kids queue to hear it whisper
   "salutations" in the dark. Wren runs
   the queue. Ash is first in line every
   day. Friends since Shelf 10. 🧦📚

7. Mr. Pinch was rehired for the Fair,
   wearing earplugs, accompanied by his
   parrot, who now says "salutations" to
   customers. Everyone agrees the parrot
   has found its calling.

WINNER: The player with more points!
EVERYONE: wins, because the ghost was a
cartoon spider biography the whole time. 👻🕷️😂

📚 GLOSSARY 词汇表

- whisper / whispering = 低语 🤫
- flicker (lights) = 灯光闪烁
- shadow = 影子
- motion sensor = 运动感应器
- sensitivity = 灵敏度（Wren按到最高的那个 😂）
- night guard = 夜班保安
- quit = 辞职
- salutations = 问候（夏洛的网里蜘蛛的名台词！）
- haunted = 闹鬼的
- replacement pair = 替换的一双（紫色手织袜 🧦）
