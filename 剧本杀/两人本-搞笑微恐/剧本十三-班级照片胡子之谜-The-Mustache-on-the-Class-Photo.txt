═══════════════════════════════════════════════════
   THE CASE OF THE MUSTACHE ON THE CLASS PHOTO 🖍️
   (A 2-Player Comedy Mystery Script)
   
   班级照片上的胡子之谜 —— 英文两人搞笑剧本杀
   Age: 10+ | Players: 2 | Time: 40-60 min
   Zero ghosts. 100% silly.
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

📖 BACKGROUND (Read Aloud Together)

Yesterday was CLASS PHOTO DAY. 📸

Everyone wore their best uniform. Everyone
practiced their smile. Even Mia, who hasn't
smiled since the Potato Incident of 2024,
practiced a smile (it looked like a grimace,
but we count it).

The photo cost each family $30.
It will go in the YEARBOOK. Forever.
Your grandkids will see it. THEIR grandkids
will see it.

Today, the proofs came back from the printer.
Everything was perfect...

EXCEPT someone had drawn a MUSTACHE 🖍️
on the photographer's screen preview —
right on PRINCIPAL SNODGRASS's face.
A curly, magnificent, villain-style mustache.

The printer says the mustache is "baked in."
It cannot be removed.
The yearbook deadline is TONIGHT.

The class is in CHAOS. 😱
The two of you were the LAST ones in the
photo studio (you stayed to help carry cables).

Find out who did it. Before Principal Snodgrass
returns from her dentist appointment at 4 pm.
The clock is ticking. The mustache is curly.
Justice must be served. 🕵️

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🎮 HOW TO PLAY

1. Each player secretly reads ONLY their own
   character card. NO PEEKING! 🙈
2. Play 3 rounds (follow the Round Cards).
3. You may LIE about anything EXCEPT the two
   things marked 【CANNOT LIE】.
4. Final round: accuse each other!
5. Then read THE TRUTH together. 🎉

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🅰️ CHARACTER CARD A — RILEY (The Art Kid)

You are RILEY. You are FAMOUS for two things:
1. Being the best artist in class
2. Once drawing a mustache on a POSTER of the
   school mascot (a duck) — an event now known
   as "Duckstache Wednesday."

Everyone already suspects you. Rude! You've
been on your best behavior for 47 days straight!
You have a countdown calendar and everything!

YOUR SECRET 🤫:
You DID draw a mustache yesterday — but on
YOUR OWN HAND, as practice for drawing your
hero: a detective from your favorite cartoon
who has the world's most magnificent mustache.

The problem: the ink is PERMANENT MARKER.
It's still on your hand RIGHT NOW. If anyone
sees it, you look 100% guilty. So you've been
hiding your hand like a sneezing panda all day.
(You've been telling people you "hurt your
wrist doing hero stuff.")

【CANNOT LIE #1】You DID draw a mustache
yesterday — somewhere.

【CANNOT LIE #2】You did NOT draw on the
photographer's screen.

YOUR TASKS (1 point each):
□ Hide your permanent-marker mustache hand
□ Convince Casey you're innocent (again 🙄)
□ Find out why Casey was laughing alone
  in the corner after photo day
□ Discover what Casey's phone screensaver is
□ Bonus +3: figure out the FULL truth of
  who made the mustache

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🅱️ CHARACTER CARD B — CASEY (The Tech Kid)

You are CASEY. You helped the photographer
carry cables because you love tech stuff.
You know how the photo preview screen works
(BECAUSE you watched a 45-minute video about
it last week. Normal behavior.)

EXHIBIT against you: Sam says you were
"laughing suspiciously at your phone" right
after photo day ended. Suspicious how?
"Like a villain," according to Sam.

YOUR SECRET 🤫:
You were laughing at your phone because your
PET HAMSTER, Kevin, escaped AGAIN. Your dad
sent you a photo of Kevin inside the cereal
box, staring at the camera like a tiny fuzzy
criminal. It's your screensaver now. It's
hilarious. You cannot stop laughing at it.

BUT — here's the real problem — while you
were laughing, you accidentally LEANED on
the photographer's table, and your
CHOCOLATE FINGERPRINT 🍫 got on the tablet
screen (you had a chocolate croissant at
break, no regrets).

The fingerprint is RIGHT WHERE the mustache
appeared. It looks BAD. Chocolate + mustache
location = prime suspect.

【CANNOT LIE #1】You DID touch the tablet
screen (accidentally!).

【CANNOT LIE #2】You did NOT draw the mustache.

YOUR TASKS (1 point each):
□ Explain the chocolate fingerprint (without
  sounding guilty)
□ Convince Riley you're innocent
□ Find out what Riley is hiding under that
  "injured" hand
□ Discover what Riley drew yesterday
□ Bonus +3: figure out the FULL truth of
  who made the mustache

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🔁 ROUND 1 — THE INTERROGATION (10 min)

Ask each other:
1. What were you doing in the studio after
   everyone left? EXACTLY. In order.
2. Explain your "hand injury," Riley. 🤨
3. Explain your "villain laughing," Casey. 🤨

💡 CLUE CARD 1 (read aloud):
"The mustache on the screen was drawn with
the photographer's OWN stylus — which was
found... back in its holder. Neatly.
Whoever did it TIDIED UP after themselves."

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🔁 ROUND 2 — THE EVIDENCE (10 min)

💡 CLUE CARD 2 (read aloud):
"Security camera (audio only) caught a voice
near the studio at 3:15 pm saying:
'Ha! Classic.' — followed by slurping sounds.
Slurping. Like... a juice box? 🧃"

💡 CLUE CARD 3 (read aloud):
"The photographer, Mr. Zoom (real name, poor
guy), admits HE drew it. Wait, WHAT?
He says: 'I was testing the draw function!
I drew a mustache on the preview to test the
stylus! I do it at EVERY school! I forgot
to undo it before printing! I've done this
at 14 schools! FOURTEEN!' 😭"

...Wait, so the case is solved? NOT SO FAST.

Mr. Zoom refuses to admit this to Principal
Snodgrass, because he's terrified of her
(everyone is). He'll only confess if someone
shows him kindness first. The yearbook still
needs saving.

Ask each other:
1. So... if Mr. Zoom did it, what do we do?
2. Should we protect him? He's nice but he
   caused this mess.
3. Also, Riley, SERIOUSLY, show your hand.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🔁 ROUND 3 — FINAL ACCUSATION (5 min)

Each player declares:
"I accuse you of the mustache because
[reasons]! And I ALSO think you secretly
[guess their secret]!"

Reveal:
- Did you guess each other's secrets? (+2 each)
- Did either of you figure out the FULL truth
  (including Mr. Zoom AND why he'll confess)? (+3)

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🎉 THE TRUTH (Read Aloud Together)

1. Mr. Zoom drew the mustache HIMSELF while
   testing his stylus. He does it at every
   school. He has never once remembered to
   press "undo." Fourteen schools. Fourteen
   mustaches. He is, technically, a serial
   mustache offender.

2. The "Ha! Classic" + slurping? That was
   Mr. Zoom himself, juice-boxing in
   self-congratulation after his mustache
   turned out "extra curly this time."

3. At 4 pm, you both approached Principal
   Snodgrass TOGETHER. Instead of accusing
   each other, you told her the truth and
   asked her to be nice to Mr. Zoom, because
   "he looks like a sad umbrella when he's
   scared." She laughed for the first time
   in recorded history. Mr. Zoom confessed
   everything, crying into his juice box.

4. The photo was reprinted. Mustache-free.
   BUT — the yearbook committee secretly kept
   one mustache version, and it now hangs in
   the staff room labeled "PRINCIPAL SNODGRASS,
   ALTERNATE UNIVERSE." She pretends to hate
   it. She visits it daily. 👑

5. Riley's marker mustache finally faded
   after 9 days of scrubbing. Duckstache
   Wednesday remains legendary. Kevin the
   hamster was found in a cereal box, arrested
   by Casey's dad, and sentenced to 3 days
   in the hamster ball. He shows no remorse.

WINNER: The player with more points!
EVERYONE: wins, because the yearbook was saved
and Principal Snodgrass laughed. 📸✨

📚 GLOSSARY 词汇表

- mustache = 胡子（八字胡那种）
- proof (printing) = 校样/预览稿
- yearbook = 年鉴/毕业纪念册
- deadline = 截止时间
- stylus = 触控笔
- suspect / prime suspect = 嫌疑人/头号嫌疑人
- evidence = 证据
- confess / confession = 招供
- permanent marker = 永久记号笔（洗不掉的那种）
- no remorse = 毫无悔意 (Kevin the hamster)
