═══════════════════════════════════════════════════
   THE CASE OF THE MISSING CLASS PET 🐹
   (A 2-Player Comedy Mystery Script)
   
   班宠仓鼠失踪案 —— 英文两人搞笑剧本杀
   Age: 10+ | Players: 2 | Time: 40-60 min
   Zero ghosts. One missing potato. 100% silly.
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

📖 BACKGROUND (Read Aloud Together)

Meet SIR Nugget. 🐹

Sir Nugget is the Year 5 class pet. He is:
- A hamster of great ambition
- Slightly overweight (the vet used the word
  "thicc" by accident and the class never
  recovered)
- Famous school-wide for once escaping and
  living in the ceiling for 3 days, during
  which he became a legend and got a knighthood
  (a paper medal taped to his cage)

This morning, Sir Nugget's cage is OPEN.
And Sir Nugget is... GONE.

But wait. It's weirder than that.

In his place sits:
A POTATO. 🥔

Not just any potato. A potato with GOOGLEY
EYES glued on, wearing Sir Nugget's paper
medal, positioned in his little hammock
like it owns the place.

There's also a note:

  "Sir Nugget has retired to the countryside.
   He is happy. Do not look for him.
   He left you this potato as a replacement.
   Its name is Baron Fry.
        — A Friend"

The class is in UPROAR. Sasha cried for
20 minutes. Then looked at Baron Fry and
said "he's kind of cute though" and cried
again because of the guilt.

The teacher, Mr. Wobbleknees (real name:
Mr. Wu, but nobody can pronounce it and
this is what the class voted to call him,
he's given up), has given YOU TWO until
3:30 pm to solve the case.

The suspects? You two were on CLASS PET
DUTY this week (feeding, water, compliments).
Convenient. VERY convenient. 🕵️

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🎮 HOW TO PLAY

1. Each player secretly reads ONLY their own
   character card. NO PEEKING! 🙈
2. Play 3 rounds (follow the Round Cards).
3. You may LIE about anything EXCEPT the two
   things marked 【CANNOT LIE】.
4. Final round: accuse each other!
5. Then read THE TRUTH together. 🎉

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🅰️ CHARACTER CARD A — MAX (The Overprotective Pet Parent)

You are MAX. You love Sir Nugget more than
some of your relatives (the boring ones).
You have 340 photos of him on your tablet.
You speak to him in a voice you're not
proud of.

You've been WORRIED about him lately:

YOUR SECRET 🤫:
Yesterday at lunch, you overheard two Year 6
kids laughing about a "HAMSTER RACE" they
were planning — a secret race in the gym
at lunchtime, where kids bring hamsters
and bet POTATO CHIPS (the currency of the
underground hamster economy, apparently).

You FREAKED. Sir Nugget is not built for
racing! He's built for sitting! He's a
philosopher, not an athlete!

So yesterday after school, you took Sir
Nugget HOME with you for safekeeping.
He's currently in a temporary cage in your
closet, living his best life with premium
sunflower seeds. The plan: return him
Monday, race-proof and smug.

But this morning — SOMEONE ELSE made him
"disappear" with a potato?! Now you look
GUILTY of a crime you... technically also
committed but for GOOD REASONS. If anyone
finds out you took him home, you're done.
Also your parents don't know there's a
hamster in the closet. It's a whole thing.

【CANNOT LIE #1】Sir Nugget is currently
at YOUR house.

【CANNOT LIE #2】You did NOT make the potato
swap or write the note.

YOUR TASKS (1 point each):
□ Hide the fact that Sir Nugget is in
  your closet (at all costs)
□ Find out who the potato artist is
□ Figure out why Leo has glue on his sleeve
□ Discover what "operation snack
  distraction" means
□ Bonus +3: figure out the FULL truth

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🅱️ CHARACTER CARD B — LEO (The Chaotic Artist)

You are LEO. You are a CHAOS GENIUS. Your
art teacher says you have "vision" and
"absolutely no impulse control." Both true.

Yesterday, you ALSO noticed Sir Nugget's
cage was empty (Max took him, though you
don't know that). You had a brilliant,
terrible, brilliant idea:

YOUR SECRET 🤫:
You made the potato swap! You glued googly
eyes on a potato ("Baron Fry" — named after
your favorite snack), put on Sir Nugget's
medal, wrote the note, and set it all up
this morning at 7:15 am.

WHY?! Because — and follow the logic —
the class would be sad about Sir Nugget
being "kidnapped," and sad people need
CHEERING UP, and what's more cheering than
a googly-eyed potato in a hammock?
EXACTLY. You're basically a hero.

Also, minor detail: you may have promised
the Year 6 kids that Sir Nugget would
"definitely appear" at the hamster race
today at lunch, because they paid you
5 potato chip bags to "arrange" it. You
then couldn't find Sir Nugget, panicked,
and the potato was Plan B. The race is at
LUNCH TODAY. It is now 11 am. You are
sweating.

The glue on your sleeve is from the googly
eyes. You've told people it's "art skin."
Nobody has accepted this explanation.

【CANNOT LIE #1】You DID make the potato
swap and write the note.

【CANNOT LIE #2】You DID take 5 bags of
potato chips from Year 6 kids.

YOUR TASKS (1 point each):
□ Hide your connection to the hamster race
  (if Max guesses it, +2 to Max)
□ Find out where Sir Nugget ACTUALLY is
□ Get Max to explain why he keeps checking
  his phone and whispering "stay calm"
□ Successfully pitch Baron Fry as a
  permanent class pet #2
□ Bonus +3: figure out the FULL truth

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🔁 ROUND 1 — THE INTERROGATION (10 min)

Ask each other:
1. When did you last see Sir Nugget? Be
   specific. Time. Location. Eye contact.
2. Leo, explain the glue. And don't say
   "art skin."
3. Max, why did you whisper "stay calm"
   to your backpack this morning? 🤨

💡 CLUE CARD 1 (read aloud):
"Security camera (the one by the library
that only records the hallway ceiling —
budget cuts) caught at 7:15 am: a figure
carrying something round. The figure did
a little 'ta-da' dance after placing it.
The dance was, per Coach Dingle's expert
analysis, 'unmistakably Leo.'"

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🔁 ROUND 2 — THE EVIDENCE (10 min)

💡 CLUE CARD 2 (read aloud):
"The note's paper matches the art room's
special gold-edged paper. Only one student
is allowed to use the gold paper unsupervised:
the art teacher's 'vision' student. Leo.
ALSO: the note's ink smells faintly of
potato chips. Fancy ink? No. Chip dust
fingers? Yes. 🥔"

💡 CLUE CARD 3 (read aloud):
"At morning break, three Year 6 students
were overheard in the corridor:
'Did Leo deliver the hamster?'
'He said 12:30 in the gym.'
'If a potato shows up instead, we want our
chips back. ALL FIVE BAGS.'
They then did a secret handshake that was,
frankly, way too complicated."

Ask each other:
1. What is happening at 12:30 in the gym?!
2. WHERE IS SIR NUGGET, actually?!
3. Is it just me or is Baron Fry growing
   on you? 🥔

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🔁 ROUND 3 — FINAL ACCUSATION (5 min)

Each player declares:
"I accuse you of the Great Hamster
Disappearance because [reasons]! And I
ALSO think you secretly [guess their
secret]!"

Reveal:
- Guessed each other's secrets? (+2 each)
- Figured out the FULL truth (closet,
  chips, race, potato logic)? (+3)

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🎉 THE TRUTH (Read Aloud Together)

1. Sir Nugget was NEVER in danger. Max took
   him home Tuesday night to protect him
   from the hamster race. He is currently
   in Max's closet on his third day of
   "witness protection," having gained
   weight AGAIN (premium seeds, no regrets).

2. Leo, unable to find Sir Nugget for the
   race he'd promised (for 5 bags of potato
   chips — an advance payment, he spent
   them immediately, classic Leo), created
   Baron Fry as a decoy and a work of art.
   He stands by both decisions.

3. At 12:30, the Year 6 kids arrived at the
   gym and were presented with Baron Fry.
   The room went silent. Then the tallest
   Year 6 kid picked up the potato, studied
   it, and said: "This is the greatest thing
   I've ever seen. The race is now a POTATO
   RACE." They rolled Baron Fry down a ramp.
   He came second. They gave him a medal
   (a bottle cap). Baron Fry now has two
   medals, one borrowed, one earned.

4. You two solved it together at 3:15 pm.
   Max confessed about the closet. Leo
   confessed about everything else (chips,
   note, glue, the whole beautiful disaster).
   Mr. Wobbleknees laughed so hard he had
   to sit down on a tiny chair, which was
   also funny, which made it worse.

5. Sir Nugget returned Monday to a hero's
   welcome. He was given a second medal
   (surviving the closet). He now has more
   medals than achievements, and honestly,
   same goals.

6. Baron Fry was adopted as class pet #2.
   He requires no food, no water, and no
   cleaning. The class voted him "Most
   Low-Maintenance" by a landslide. Sasha
   still says he's "kind of cute." She was
   right. 🥔

7. The Year 6 hamster race was permanently
   replaced by the Annual Potato Roll.
   Attendance: 100%. Chips: consumed.
   Legends: made.

WINNER: The player with more points!
EVERYONE: wins, because there's now a
retired hamster, a racing potato, and a
classroom with two pets, one of which
is produce. 🥔✨

📚 GLOSSARY 词汇表

- class pet = 班级宠物
- overprotective = 过度保护的
- googly eyes = 活动眼睛（手工贴片那种）
- decoy = 替身/诱饵
- witness protection = 证人保护计划（Max的衣柜版）
- underground economy = 地下经济（薯片货币 😂）
- impulse control = 冲动控制（Leo没有的东西）
- low-maintenance = 好养的/不费心的
- produce = 农产品（Baron Fry 是农产品 🥔）
- landslide (vote) = 压倒性胜利
