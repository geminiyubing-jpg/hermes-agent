═══════════════════════════════════════════════════
   THE CASE OF THE TALENT SHOW SABOTAGE 🎤
   (A 2-Player Comedy Mystery Script)
   
   才艺表演破坏案 —— 英文两人搞笑剧本杀
   Age: 10+ | Players: 2 | Time: 40-60 min
   Zero ghosts. 100% backstage drama.
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

📖 BACKGROUND (Read Aloud Together)

TONIGHT is the ANNUAL TALENT SHOW. 🎤

450 people in the audience. Parents,
teachers, and — for reasons nobody
understands — the mayor, who attends
every year and cries at every act. Every.
Single. Act. (Last year he cried at a
yo-yo demonstration. It was "the string,
so much dedication in the string.")

You two are the HEADLINE ACTS:

Act 9: JESSE & THE JESSES — actually just
       one kid named Jesse, who does magic.
       (The name sounded cooler with "and
       the Jesses." There are no other
       Jesses. Jesse knows. Jesse is at
       peace with it.)

Act 10: THE SYNCHRONIZED SILLY STRING
        SISTERS — actually two sisters,
        Harper and... Harper's little
        sister Pearl, who does a "comedy
        violin" act. (It's violin, but she
        stops dramatically to tell jokes.
        The jokes are about cheese. They
        are incredible.)

At 5:00 pm, 90 minutes before showtime,
DISASTER:

Act 9's MAGIC PROPS — the trick cards,
the fake flower, the multiplying foam
balls — have been REPLACED with vegetables.
A zucchini where the magic wand was.
A celery where the appearing cane was.
The trick cards are now a bag of spinach.

Act 10's VIOLIN — Pearl's precious
violin — has a GOGGLE-EYED RUBBER DUCK
tied to it with a sparkly ribbon.

AND — the sound system will only play
one track: "Old MacDonald Had a Farm,"
at maximum volume, no matter what button
is pressed. The tech kid has been trying
for an hour. He's sitting in the corner
now, hugging his knees. The farm song
plays on. 🎵

Someone is sabotaging the talent show.
Someone with vegetables, a duck, and a
grudge.

You two are the top suspects — you're
the headliners with the most to lose...
and the only two with backstage access
all afternoon.

Showtime: 6:30 pm. Find the saboteur.
Save the show. 🕵️🎤

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🎮 HOW TO PLAY

1. Each player secretly reads ONLY their own
   character card. NO PEEKING! 🙈
2. Play 3 rounds (follow the Round Cards).
3. You may LIE about anything EXCEPT the two
   things marked 【CANNOT LIE】.
4. Final round: accuse each other!
5. Then read THE TRUTH together. 🎉

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🅰️ CHARACTER CARD A — JESSE (The Magician)

You are JESSE, of "Jesse & the Jesses."
You've done magic for 3 years. You're good.
Like, "birthday parties pay you in pizza"
good. Tonight is your BIGGEST show ever.

But here's the thing nobody knows:

YOUR SECRET 🤫:
Your grand finale trick — "The Vanishing
Bandana" — has been FAILING all week.
The bandana doesn't vanish. It just sits
there. Being a bandana. Mocking you.

The problem: the trick needs a specific
FAKE bandana (collapsible). Yours broke,
and the replacement hasn't arrived.
You've been practicing the "showmanship"
part anyway: dramatic gestures, mysterious
face, jazz hands at the end.

Your PLAN B: do the trick with a REAL
bandana and just... throw it really hard
at a bucket while shouting "BEHOLD."
Physics will do the rest. It's not magic,
it's sports, but the audience doesn't
know that.

Yesterday, someone saw you backstage
arguing with a celery stick (you were
practicing an angry magician speech and
the celery was a stand-in prop for the
wand). That someone now thinks you're
"a vegetable person." Whatever THAT means.

ALSO: your show jacket has a mysterious
MUSTARD STAIN that appeared at 4:45 pm.
You don't eat mustard. You don't even
LIKE mustard. Where did this stain come
from?? You've been frantically trying to
hide it all afternoon.

【CANNOT LIE #1】Your finale trick is
currently broken / being replaced with
"throwing a bandana really hard."

【CANNOT LIE #2】You did NOT sabotage
anyone's props.

YOUR TASKS (1 point each):
□ Hide the bandana disaster (if Harper
  guesses the "Plan B is sports" plan,
  +2 to Harper)
□ Explain the mustard stain mystery
□ Find out why Harper has spinach in
  her violin case
□ Discover who smells like farm animals 🦆
□ Bonus +3: figure out the FULL truth

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🅱️ CHARACTER CARD B — HARPER (The Violinist's Big Sister)

You are HARPER. You play "second violin,
first comedian" in the act — you do the
jokes while Pearl plays. (The deal: Pearl
hates talking, you love talking. Perfect
crime. Perfect act.)

You've been a bit STRESSED lately:

YOUR SECRET 🤫:
You may have... sort of... kind of...
AUDITIONED to host the whole talent show
last month, and got REJECTED. The
committee said you were "too much." TOO
MUCH?! You've been doing comedy about
this rejection for three weeks. It's your
best material. The cheese jokes? Amateurs.
The hosting rejection jokes? ART.

And on the rejection letter — which you
keep in your violin case for motivation —
there's a spinach leaf pressed against it
like a bookmark. A SPINACH LEAF. You don't
even remember putting it there. It's been
there so long it's basically fossilized.

THIS MORNING, you were seen backstage at
7:00 am (before anyone arrived) doing
something suspicious near the sound desk.
Truth: you were secretly recording a
"pilot episode" of your own talk show,
"Backstage with Harper," using the talent
show's equipment. Episode one features
you interviewing a broom. It's nine
minutes. The broom says nothing. It's
riveting.

If the committee finds out you used the
sound system, you're banned from
EVERYTHING forever.

【CANNOT LIE #1】You DID use the sound
system this morning (for... purposes).

【CANNOT LIE #2】You did NOT sabotage
anyone's props or tie a duck to the violin.

YOUR TASKS (1 point each):
□ Hide the secret talk show (if Jesse
  guesses it, +2 to Jesse)
□ Find out what's really going on with
  Jesse's finale
□ Figure out where the mustard stain
  came from
□ Discover who replaced the magic props
  with vegetables
□ Bonus +3: figure out the FULL truth

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🔁 ROUND 1 — THE INTERROGATION (10 min)

Ask each other:
1. Where were you between 4:30 and 5:00 pm,
   when the sabotage was discovered?
2. Jesse: why is there mustard on you?
   You said, quote, "I don't even like
   mustard."
3. Harper: why does the sound system ONLY
   play "Old MacDonald"? What did you DO
   at that sound desk this morning? 👀

💡 CLUE CARD 1 (read aloud):
"The sabotage happened between 4:40 and
4:50 pm. The props room smelled like...
a petting zoo. 🦆 Witnesses (one kid
eating crisps in the corner) report
hearing someone humming 'Old MacDonald'
HAPPILY while swapping the props. Not
sarcastically. HAPPILY. While quacking
occasionally."

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🔁 ROUND 2 — THE EVIDENCE (10 min)

💡 CLUE CARD 2 (read aloud):
"The rubber duck on Pearl's violin has a
tag: 'PROPERTY OF THE MAPLE STREET PETTING
ZOO — IF FOUND, THIS DUCK HAS ESCAPED
(twice before).' The petting zoo is run by
Farmer Dunbar... whose DAUGHTER is in
Act 3 of tonight's show: 'Porky's Grand
Adventure' — a pig-riding dog act. Wait.
A pig-riding dog act?! And the headliners
are a magician and violinists?! WHO
APPROVED THIS LINEUP?!"

💡 CLUE CARD 3 (read aloud):
"Further evidence: at 4:45 pm, Farmer
Dunbar himself was seen backstage —
delivering 'party subs' for the performers.
The subs: ALL MUSTARD. Extra mustard.
'He said no mayo, only mustard, it's
tradition,' said the sandwich shop owner,
who thought this was weird enough to
mention."

Ask each other:
1. So the farmer... fed everyone mustard
   subs... while someone sabotaged the
   headliners... to make a PIG-RIDING DOG
   ACT the star?!
2. Who benefits if Acts 9 and 10 flop?
3. Seriously, whose duck is this? 🦆

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🔁 ROUND 3 — FINAL ACCUSATION (5 min)

Each player declares:
"I accuse you of talent show sabotage
because [reasons]! And I ALSO think you
secretly [guess their secret]!"

Reveal:
- Guessed each other's secrets? (+2 each)
- Figured out the FULL truth (Act 3, the
  duck, the mustard, the sound system)? (+3)

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🎉 THE TRUTH (Read Aloud Together)

1. The saboteur: BISCUIT. 🐕
   The dog. From Act 3. The pig-riding dog.

2. Hear us out. Biscuit's trainer —
   Farmer Dunbar's daughter, TILLY —
   wanted Act 3 to be the headline. So she
   taught Biscuit a new "trick": sneak
   backstage, swap the props with the
   vegetable basket (Tilly provided),
   tie the duck (Biscuit's own toy, a
   petting zoo escapee) to the violin,
   and press ONE special button on the
   sound system — the one Tilly labeled
   with a sticker of a barn.

3. The barn sticker? Tilly made "the
   button that plays farm sounds" months
   ago for her act. Biscuit pressed it.
   The tech kid spent an hour fighting
   a dog's sticker. The farm song played
   on.

4. The mustard: while Biscuit did the
   sabotage, Farmer Dunbar was handing
   out mustard subs. Jesse accepted one
   out of politeness, put it in their
   jacket pocket (WHY, Jesse), forgot
   about it, and sat on it. Case of the
   mustard stain: closed. It was
   self-inflicted. The sub, not the
   sabotage.

5. How was Biscuit caught? Paw prints.
   On the sound desk. Tiny. Perfect.
   Also Biscuit, when confronted, did
   the trick again on command — Tilly,
   panicking, yelled "BISCUIT, RELEASE
   THE DUCK!" and Biscuit, thrilled to
   perform, released the duck. Evidence
   flew everywhere. Confession by
   squeaky toy.

6. The show went on! Jesse's bandana
   trick: the REAL fake bandana arrived
   at 6:11 pm (delivery guy ran three
   red lights, a hero). Trick worked.
   Mayor cried. Pearl's violin — duck
   removed — was beautiful. Her cheese
   joke ("Why did the cheese refuse to
   fight? It was too mature!") got the
   biggest laugh of the night. Mayor
   cried at that too.

7. Act 3 went on with one change: Biscuit
   performed the sabotage as PART of the
   act ("The World's Most Helpful Dog").
   Standing ovation. Biscuit now has an
   agent. Tilly has a detention and a
   manager (same thing, she says).

8. Harper's talk show pilot? Leaked.
   Played on the school's screen at
   lunch. The broom interview is now
   school legend. Harper was NOT
   punished — the committee said anyone
   who can make a broom interesting
   DESERVES a show. "Backstage with
   Harper" returns next season. The
   broom is coming back too. It's the
   only guest who never cancels.

WINNER: The player with more points!
EVERYONE: wins, because the dog did it,
and honestly, that's the best kind of
mystery ending there is. 🐕🎤✨

📚 GLOSSARY 词汇表

- talent show = 才艺表演
- headline act = 压轴节目
- sabotage / saboteur = 破坏/破坏者
- magician = 魔术师
- vanishing trick = 消失魔术
- showmanship = 表演技巧/台风
- jazz hands = 爵士手（演出结束的经典动作 ✋✨）
- riveting = 扣人心弦的（Harper对扫把采访的评价 😂）
- standing ovation = 起立鼓掌
- confession by squeaky toy = 挤捏玩具式招供 🦆
