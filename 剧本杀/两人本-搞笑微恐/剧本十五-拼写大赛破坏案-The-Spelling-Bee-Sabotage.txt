═══════════════════════════════════════════════════
   THE CASE OF THE SPELLING BEE SABOTAGE 🐝
   (A 2-Player Comedy Mystery Script)
   
   拼写大赛破坏案 —— 英文两人搞笑剧本杀
   Age: 10+ | Players: 2 | Time: 40-60 min
   Zero ghosts. 100% academic chaos.
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

📖 BACKGROUND (Read Aloud Together)

The ANNUAL SPELLING BEE is tomorrow. 🐝
The prize: a GIANT trophy, a $50 book voucher,
and the eternal glory of having your name
painted on the library wall (right above
"2024 — Nobody, the bee was cancelled due
to a fire alarm caused by toast").

You two are the FINALISTS. Out of 200
students, it's down to YOU TWO. 🏆

But this morning, disaster struck:

ALL FOUR of the official spelling bee
dictionary sets — the ones with this year's
word list — have VANISHED from the library.
Every. Single. Copy.

Even worse, the LIBRARIAN, Ms. Quillfeather,
discovered a mysterious NOTE taped to the
empty shelf:

  "The bee will buzz no more.
   Study if you dare — the words are
       EVERYWHERE. 🐝"

The note smells faintly of... pickles? 🥒

Ms. Quillfeather is devastated. The principal
says if the dictionaries aren't found by
tomorrow morning, the bee is CANCELLED — and
the wall gets painted with "2026 — Nobody,
again, we're so sorry."

You two could just... let it be cancelled.
Whoever is more scared of losing might
benefit. 👀

OR you could solve the mystery, save the bee,
and face each other in a fair fight.

The night is young. The pickles are suspicious.
Find those dictionaries. 🕵️

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🎮 HOW TO PLAY

1. Each player secretly reads ONLY their own
   character card. NO PEEKING! 🙈
2. Play 3 rounds (follow the Round Cards).
3. You may LIE about anything EXCEPT the two
   things marked 【CANNOT LIE】.
4. Final round: accuse each other!
5. Then read THE TRUTH together. 🎉

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🅰️ CHARACTER CARD A — PHOENIX (The Favorite)

You are PHOENIX. You've won the spelling bee
TWO YEARS RUNNING. Your vocabulary is
legendary. You once corrected your dentist
("Actually, it's bicuspids, not molars"),
and he thanked you. Weird guy.

Everyone expects you to win again. The
pressure is IMMENSE. Your mom has already
told the neighbors. THE NEIGHBORS, Phoenix.

YOUR SECRET 🤫:
Last night, you realized you didn't actually
know how to spell "necessary." NECESSARY.
The most basic word in existence. You
panicked. You practiced it 200 times on a
sticky note and STUCK IT TO YOUR ARM to
study in the shower (the note is now a wet,
crumbling disaster in your pocket like a
sad paper ghost — NOT a real ghost, relax).

Worse: that sticky note fell out of your
pocket THIS MORNING near the library. If
anyone finds a sticky note with "NECESSARY
NECESSARY NECESSARY (one C, two S's?)"
in your handwriting... you will die of
embarrassment before the bee even starts.

AND — the dangerous part — the library
security log shows YOUR library card was
used at 7:58 am today, two minutes before
the dictionaries were discovered missing.

But you swear you were NOT in the library
at that time! Your card must have been...
borrowed?!

【CANNOT LIE #1】You genuinely CANNOT spell
"necessary" (well, you couldn't, before
the 200 shower practices).

【CANNOT LIE #2】You did NOT enter the
library this morning.

YOUR TASKS (1 point each):
□ Hide the "necessary" situation AT ALL COSTS
□ Figure out who used your library card
□ Find out why Dani smells like pickles
□ Discover where Dani was at 7:58 am
□ Bonus +3: figure out the FULL truth

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🅱️ CHARACTER CARD B — DANI (The Underdog)

You are DANI. First time in the finals.
Nobody expected you here — you beat 198
people with pure chaos energy and a lucky
streak on French-origin words (croissant,
bureaucracy, rendezvous — you know things).

You're the underdog, and everyone loves an
underdog. Except you're TERRIFIED. Your
hands sweat so much during the semifinal
that your number card dissolved slightly.

YOUR SECRET 🤫:
You have a PRE-GAME RITUAL: you eat exactly
one pickled cucumber before every competition.
For luck. Your grandpa swears by it ("It
worked in 1962, it works forever").

This morning you ate your ritual pickle at
7:50 am, right outside the library — and
then PANICKED because you saw someone
INSIDE the library through the window at
7:58 am, moving quickly between the shelves,
carrying a stack of books.

You'd recognize that silhouette anywhere:
it was... MS. QUILLFEATHER HERSELF?!
The librarian?! At 7:58 am?! Looking
shifty?!

But wait — why would the LIBRARIAN steal
the dictionaries from her OWN library?
Unless... she's sabotaging the bee?? You
smell a story. Also you smell like pickles.
Constantly. It's the pickle thing.

You haven't told anyone, because:
1. Who believes the underdog accusing
   the beloved librarian?
2. Your "evidence" is: you saw her through
   a window while holding a pickle.

【CANNOT LIE #1】You DID see someone in the
library at 7:58 am.

【CANNOT LIE #2】You eat a pickle before
every competition (grandpa's rule).

YOUR TASKS (1 point each):
□ Hide your pickle ritual (if Phoenix
  guesses it, +2 to Phoenix)
□ Reveal what you saw at 7:58 am in a way
  people actually believe
□ Find out whose handwriting is on that
  ransom-ish note 🥒
□ Get Phoenix to admit a word they can't
  spell
□ Bonus +3: figure out the FULL truth

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🔁 ROUND 1 — THE INTERROGATION (10 min)

Ask each other:
1. Where were you at 7:58 am this morning?
2. Phoenix, why does the security log show
   your card entering the library?
3. Dani, why do you smell like a deli? 🥒🤨

💡 CLUE CARD 1 (read aloud):
"The library security log shows Phoenix's
card scanned at 7:58 am. But the SECURITY
CAMERA shows the person scanning it was...
4 feet tall. Phoenix is 5'2". Also the
person was wearing a trench coat. To school.
In summer. Who wears a trench coat in
summer?? 🧥"

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🔁 ROUND 2 — THE EVIDENCE (10 min)

💡 CLUE CARD 2 (read aloud):
"The mysterious note, analyzed by the
school's handwriting enthusiast (Mr. Bok,
the janitor, who does calligraphy as a
hobby — the school is full of secrets):
'The handwriting is elegant. Well-practiced.
Old-fashioned loops. This is someone who
learned cursive PROPERLY. Also there's a
tiny ink smudge shaped like a feather.'"

💡 CLUE CARD 3 (read aloud):
"UPDATE: A Year 3 student has come forward.
He saw 'a trench coat person' this morning
hiding a stack of books behind the GYM.
When asked why he didn't say anything
earlier, he said, quote: 'I thought it was
a spy. I was respecting the spy.'"

Ask each other:
1. A trench coat, 4 feet tall, hiding books
   at the gym... who does that describe?
2. What's Ms. Quillfeather's deal?!
3. Also: Phoenix, that crumpled thing in
   your pocket... what is it? 👀

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🔁 ROUND 3 — FINAL ACCUSATION (5 min)

Each player declares:
"I accuse you of dictionary theft because
[reasons]! And I ALSO think you secretly
[guess their secret]!"

Reveal:
- Guessed each other's secrets? (+2 each)
- Guessed the FULL truth (the trench coat,
  the librarian's plan, the pickle motive)? (+3)

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🎉 THE TRUTH (Read Aloud Together)

1. The dictionary thief was... BENJAMIN.
   A Year 3 student. Four feet tall.
   Wearing his dad's trench coat because
   "spies wear these." He used Phoenix's
   library card — which Phoenix DROPPED
   yesterday near the cafeteria (the sticky
   note fell out of the same pocket, RIP).

2. Why did Benjamin steal the dictionaries?
   Because HE wanted to compete in the
   spelling bee, but you have to be Year 4+.
   His plan: if the bee gets cancelled,
   "it doesn't count that I'm not allowed
   in it." Flawless logic. He wrote the
   note himself — he copied the handwriting
   from his favorite book series about a
   detective with a feather hat (hence the
   feather-shaped smudge, he dipped the
   pen too deep, drama).

3. The pickle smell on the note? Benjamin
   found the note paper... in his pocket...
   next to his own ritual pickle sandwich.
   (Every family has traditions. The
   Quillfeathers have cursive; Benjamin's
   family has pickles.)

4. And MS. QUILLFEATHER at 7:58 am? She was
   in the library because she lives for
   that place and was reorganizing the
   fantasy section by "dragon color." She
   saw EVERYTHING — Benjamin, trench coat,
   stolen card — but said nothing because
   she wanted to see if you two could solve
   it. She's been taking notes for the
   school newspaper the whole time. Her
   headline is already written:
   "SLEUTHING FINAlists SAVE THE BEE (and a
   Year 3 boy from himself)."

5. The dictionaries were found behind the
   gym. The bee happened. It was legendary:
   it came down to a sudden-death word.
   The word was — you guessed it —
   "NECESSARY." Phoenix closed their eyes,
   thought of the shower note, and spelled
   it perfectly. One C. Two S's. Phoenix
   won by a single word... and immediately
   credited "a very dedicated shower."

6. Dani came second — but got the Sports-
   ship Award and a special prize from
   Ms. Quillfeather: a custom bookmark with
   a pickle on it. Dani framed it.

7. Benjamin was given a special job:
   official Spelling Bee Mascot. He wore
   the trench coat. He has never been
   happier. The bee buzzed on. 🐝

WINNER: The player with more points!
EVERYONE: wins, because spelling was saved,
and "necessary" was finally spelled right. ✨

📚 GLOSSARY 词汇表

- spelling bee = 拼写大赛 🐝
- finalist = 决赛选手
- sabotage = 阴谋破坏
- underdog = 黑马/不被看好的人
- ritual = 仪式（Dani的腌黄瓜仪式 🥒）
- vanish = 消失
- silhouette = 身影/剪影
- trench coat = 风衣（间谍标配 🧥）
- sudden death = 加时赛/一词定胜负
- sportsmanship = 运动员精神
