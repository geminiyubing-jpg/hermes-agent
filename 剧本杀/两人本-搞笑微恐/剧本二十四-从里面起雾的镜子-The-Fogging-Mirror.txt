═══════════════════════════════════════════════════
   THE MIRROR THAT FOGS FROM THE INSIDE 🪞
   (A 2-Player Spooky-Comedy Mystery Script)
   
   从里面起雾的镜子 —— 英文两人微恐+搞笑剧本杀
   Spooky level: 🌑🌑🌑🌑 (the creepiest
   buildup yet; softest possible landing)
   Age: 10+ | Players: 2 | Time: 40-60 min
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

📖 BACKGROUND (Read Aloud Together)

The girls' bathroom on the third floor
was renovated last month. New tiles, new
taps, and one wall of NEW MIRRORS.

Mirror #3 has a problem. 😨

It fogs up. From the INSIDE.

Not steam-fog from the outside — the
cleaners checked — but a mist that
gathers BEHIND the glass, in the depth
of the mirror, like breath on a window
from a room that doesn't exist.

It started nine days ago. The pattern:

- The fog only appears between 3:15 and
  3:45 pm. Never any other time.
- The fog always forms the SAME shape
  first: a small circle, at head height,
  like someone standing very close,
  breathing on their side of the glass.
- Then, on Wednesday, the fog formed
  WORDS. Reversed, readable from your
  side. Three words:

      LET ME OUT 😨

The school closed the bathroom. An
"OUT OF ORDER" sign went up. Which, of
course, made everything worse, because
now nobody could LOOK, and everyone
needed to look.

A Year 7 girl snuck in Thursday and
filmed the mirror for 40 minutes. At
3:27 pm the circle-fog appeared. At
3:31, new words:

      WHO IS OUT THERE

The girl answered out loud — "Fern? Is
that you? I'm scared" (she has a friend
named Fern; don't ask; teenagers) — and
the fog... wrote back:

      NOT FERN

And then, while she watched, a small
HANDPRINT appeared in the fog. From the
inside. Five fingers. Child-sized.
Pressing. 😨😨

The video has 400 views. The bathroom
is locked with an actual padlock now.
The school has called a mirror company,
a glazier, and — quietly, off the books —
a priest, who said "it's probably
condensation" and left, which nobody
found comforting.

You two have the key (the caretaker
trusts you; long story; you returned
his lost cat in October — the good
karma bank is endless).

Today, 3:15 pm approaches. You're going
in. 🪞🌙

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🎮 HOW TO PLAY

1. Each player secretly reads ONLY their
   own character card. NO PEEKING! 🙈
2. Play 3 rounds (follow the Round Cards).
3. You may LIE about anything EXCEPT the
   two things marked 【CANNOT LIE】.
4. Final round: accuse each other!
5. Then read THE TRUTH together. 🎉

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🅰️ CHARACTER CARD A — FERN (Yes, That Fern)

You are FERN. The girl in the viral
video who said "Fern? Is that you?" is
your best friend Ivy — and she was
talking to YOU, because you were late
to meet her that day, and she was
scared, and your name is apparently
now permanently attached to a haunted
bathroom legend. Excellent.

You've been quietly investigating on
your own, because everyone keeps
saying "the mirror wants FERN" and
you'd like that to stop.

YOUR SECRET 🤫:
You know something nobody else knows,
and it's big: two weeks ago — BEFORE
any of this started — you saw the
glazier who installed the mirrors.

You were in the bathroom (legally,
normally, pre-haunting) on installation
day, and the glazier's apprentice —
a teenager, maybe 17, nervous — was
carrying Mirror #3. He tripped. On
nothing. He just tripped (you've since
realized the tile there is uneven —
renovation quality, a scandal for
another day). He caught the mirror
before it hit the ground, checked it,
seemed relieved... then held it up to
the light, frowned, and whispered to
it: "sorry, little guy."

SORRY, LITTLE GUY.

Then he installed it anyway. You watched
him do it. You said nothing (you were
12 and eating a cereal bar; priorities).

You've also noticed — small thing,
maybe nothing — that the fog words
aren't perfectly formed. The letters
are shaky. Like someone writing with
their non-dominant hand. Or someone
very tired. Or someone writing on
glass with a finger... through a GAP
in the mirror's backing.

【CANNOT LIE #1】You saw the apprentice
trip and say "sorry, little guy" to
Mirror #3.

【CANNOT LIE #2】You were in the
bathroom at installation day and said
nothing.

YOUR TASKS (1 point each):
□ Clear your name from the legend
  ("the mirror wants Fern" is RUINING
  your life)
□ Reveal the glazier evidence
□ Figure out why 3:15-3:45 pm
□ Find out what Ivy knows that she's
  not telling (she's been weird all
  week)
□ Bonus +3: figure out the FULL truth

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🅱️ CHARACTER CARD B — IVY (The Filmer With Guilt)

You are IVY. You took the viral video.
"Fern? Is that you?" — that's you. Your
voice is now a school legend and you
have mixed feelings (mostly horror).

But here's what nobody knows about
that video:

YOUR SECRET 🤫:
You didn't sneak in Thursday just to
film. You went in because you'd been
PAYING ATTENTION to something everyone
else missed, and you needed to test it.

The fog words appear 3:15-3:45 pm. You
checked the school schedule: 3:15-3:45
is when the CLEANING STAFF takes their
break — in the room directly behind
the mirror wall. The STAFF ROOM. The
mirror wall is shared with the staff
room. The "inside" of the mirror is...
the staff room side.

And on Wednesday — the day "LET ME OUT"
appeared — you were walking past the
staff room at 3:30 and heard, through
the door, a small voice, frustrated,
muttering: "stupid... glue... let go...
I just need ONE... ohh the glue won't...

(let me out) "...why won't this glue
come off..."

The mirror words and the muttering
were THE SAME SENTENCES. Simultaneous.
The person in the staff room was
saying out loud what the mirror was
"writing."

You realized — standing in that
hallway, heart exploding — that the
"ghost" was in the STAFF ROOM, talking
to itself, while somehow ALSO writing
on the bathroom mirror. You did not
go in (you are brave on camera, less
so in person; fair enough).

You haven't told anyone, because the
conclusion is either (a) a staff
member is writing on mirrors through
the wall, which is insane, or (b) an
actual ghost is in the staff room,
which is worse. Neither option has
improved your sleep.

ALSO — the REAL guilt — on Tuesday,
before any of the words appeared, YOU
were in the bathroom when the circle-
fog first formed, and you ran out
screaming without checking if anyone
needed help. You've been wondering
all week if someone DID need help.
Like, help-help. Real help. What if
the ghost is trapped? What if it's
not a ghost? What if you ran away
from someone? 😰

【CANNOT LIE #1】You heard the same
words through the staff room door at
the same time the mirror wrote them.

【CANNOT LIE #2】You ran out on Tuesday
without checking whether someone
needed help.

YOUR TASKS (1 point each):
□ Share the staff room discovery
□ Deal with your Tuesday guilt (did
  someone need help?)
□ Get Fern to explain "sorry, little
  guy" (you've heard the rumor of the
  rumor; get the source)
□ Decide: do we go into the staff
  room, or wait at the mirror at
  3:15?
□ Bonus +3: figure out the FULL truth

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🔁 ROUND 1 — THE INTERROGATION (10 min)

Ask each other:
1. Compare timelines: when did each
   "haunting" element first appear?
2. Ivy: "NOT FERN." The mirror
   answered you. Walk me through
   exactly what happened, in order.
3. Fern: everyone says "the mirror
   wants Fern." Why did that start?
   What did YOU do?

💡 CLUE CARD 1 (read aloud):
"The mirror wall, examined by the
caretaker from the STAFF ROOM side
today: behind Mirror #3's position
hangs a large, old, framed STAFF
ACHIEVEMENT AWARD from 2009, reading
'MOST IMPROVED KITCHEN 2009.' The
award frame's backing paper is torn
at one corner. Behind the mirror,
where the wall meets the frame, there
is a gap. A small, deliberate-looking
gap. The caretaker reports: 'That
award's been behind that wall since
before the renovation. The mirror
fitters said the wall was "weird"
back there. They said there was
"already a hole" behind the old
mirror. They didn't ask. Fitters
don't ask. They fit.'"

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🔁 ROUND 2 — THE EVIDENCE (10 min)

💡 CLUE CARD 2 (read aloud):
"The glazier's company was called.
The receptionist, chatty, revealed:
'Third floor bathroom? Oh, we almost
didn't install those. Our apprentice
DROPPED one — well, caught it — and
there was a defect. A blemish behind
the glass, they said, like a bubble
in the silvering. Standard policy is
replace it. But the apprentice begged
the boss to keep it. Said it was
"his best work." Said the blemish
"looked like a hand." Said — and I
quote, this kid is a POET — "every
mirror deserves a ghost." The boss
laughed and let it slide. That was
a month ago. Why? ...Why are you
asking? Is this about the fog? People
have been calling. Is the ghost
REAL?' 😨"

💡 CLUE CARD 3 (read aloud):
"The staff room, staked out at 3:20
pm yesterday by the headteacher
herself (she doesn't believe in
ghosts; she believes in BREAK ROOM
SHENANIGANS): during the exact fog
window, the only staff member in the
room was the new assistant caretaker,
MOSS — a tiny, cheerful 22-year-old
who fixes everything, loves the job,
and has hands that are, per the
headteacher, 'permanently glittery.
Permanently. I've asked. He says it's
"craft damage." He does crafts at
lunch. In the staff room. Alone. With
the door closed.'

The headteacher also noted — and
filed under 'things I cannot say out
loud' — that Moss talks to himself
constantly while crafting. Kindly.
Encouragingly. 'Almost like he's...
talking to someone. On the other side
of something.'"

Ask each other:
1. A defective mirror with a hand-
   shaped blemish. A staff room. A
   gap in the wall. A glittery
   craftsman. 3:15-3:45. Assemble
   it. ASSEMBLE IT NOW.
2. WHO IS FERN— I mean, what does
   "LET ME OUT" mean?!
3. Ivy... your Tuesday guilt. What
   if you didn't run away from a
   ghost? What if you ran away from
   a PERSON? What is MOSS doing?! 😨

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🔁 ROUND 3 — FINAL ACCUSATION (5 min)

Each player declares:
"I accuse you of being the Mirror
Ghost because [reasons]! And I ALSO
think you secretly [guess their
secret]!"

Reveal:
- Guessed each other's secrets? (+2 each)
- Figured out the FULL truth (Moss,
  the gap, the glue, the letters)? (+3)

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🎉 THE TRUTH (Read Aloud Together)

1. The "ghost" is MOSS. The tiny,
   glittery, cheerful assistant
   caretaker. And the full story is
   the softest thing this school has
   ever produced.

2. Moss does crafts at lunch. His
   speciality: TINY RESIN DIORAMAS —
   miniature scenes in little glass
   frames. His masterpiece-in-progress:
   a tiny bedroom. With a tiny bed,
   tiny rug, tiny fairy lights that
   ACTUALLY LIGHT UP (he's an electri-
   cian's son; the lights are real;
   it's frankly incredible).

3. The problem: the tiny bedroom's
   glass front kept cracking. He
   needed a small, sturdy, perfectly
   clear pane. And one day, checking
   the gap behind the bathroom mirror
   wall (his job — the "already a
   hole" gap), he noticed something:
   through the gap, at exactly the
   right angle... his staff room
   crafting table was reflected in
   the bathroom mirror's backing
   gap. No wait — simpler:

   He discovered that the gap in the
   wall connects almost to the back
   of Mirror #3 — and that if he
   breathed warm air through the gap,
   it fogged the space between the
   mirror's glass and its silvered
   backing (the defect from the
   apprentice's drop had left a tiny
   pocket there). The fog showed
   THROUGH. From the bathroom side,
   it looked like fog INSIDE the
   mirror.

4. It started as an accident. Then
   he noticed people's faces when
   they saw it. Delight. Wonder.
   SCREAMING, yes, but joyful
   screaming. This school was
   stressed (exam season). Moss, a
   gentle soul, made a choice:

   He started doing it on purpose.
   3:15-3:45, lunch break, breath
   through the gap, draw on the
   inside of the mirror with a
   pencil-length wire he'd fed
   through months ago (the "shaky
   letters" — writing mirrored
   letters through a wall with a
   wire, sight unseen, is HARD;
   he practiced for hours; the
   handprint was his actual hand
   pressed to the gap's pocket,
   sized down by the pocket —
   child-sized, because Moss has
   child-sized hands and everyone
   forgot that detail).

5. "LET ME OUT" / "WHO IS OUT
   THERE" / "NOT FERN" — Moss had
   overheard students saying the
   bathroom felt "sad and empty"
   since the renovation removed
   the plants. He wanted to write
   something friendly. His first
   choice: "HELLO FRIENDS." It
   came out through the wire as
   "LET ME OUT" (wire writing is
   hard; the letters betrayed him;
   he was horrified; he tried to
   fix it; it got worse).

   "NOT FERN" was him, panicking,
   trying to say "NOT SCARY" — the
   wire slipped. He then spent the
   whole night wondering who Fern
   is and feeling terrible.

6. And the Tuesday guilt — Ivy's
   guilt — the first circle-fog,
   before the words: that WAS an
   accident. Moss had dropped his
   glue behind the crafting table
   and said "ohh the glue won't
   come off" out loud, warm breath
   sighing through the gap — fog
   circle — while Ivy was at the
   mirror. Nobody needed help.
   Nobody was trapped. Ivy ran
   from a sigh and a glue com-
   plaint. She is forgiven. She
   has also privately apologized
   to the mirror, which is now a
   normal thing to do apparently.

7. At 3:15 pm today, you two sat
   in the bathroom (door open,
   caretaker supervising, head-
   teacher watching through the
   staff room door — full govern-
   ance, this school learned). The
   fog circle formed. Fern knocked
   on the glass: three knocks.

   From the staff room: silence.
   Then a tiny voice: "...you
   figured it out?"

   Then Moss's face appeared at
   the gap — one eye, enormo in
   the mirror's depth like a
   friendly giant's — and he
   whispered: "I'm sorry about
   the Fern thing. I was trying
   to write NOT SCARY. Hi, Fern.
   I'm Moss. Your name is very
   hard to write with a wire."

8. The verdict: the school didn't
   punish anyone. The bathroom
   reopened. The mirror stays —
   now officially "The Friendly
   Mirror," and every Friday 3:15,
   Moss writes one word through
   the wall. Last week: "BRAVE."
   This week: "FERN OK?" — he's
   still practicing; the school
   voted to keep the imperfections.
   The tiny resin bedroom won a
   regional craft award; it's
   displayed in the library under
   glass; kids press their hands
   to the case and wave at the
   tiny bed. Moss waves back,
   3:15, every day, through the
   wall, where nobody sees, which
   is the point of most kindness.

WINNER: The player with more points!
EVERYONE: wins, because the ghost was
a glittery handyman writing badly with
a wire, for the sweetest reason on
earth. 🪞✨

- glazier = 装玻璃的师傅
- apprentice = 学徒
- resin diorama = 树脂微缩场景 🎨
