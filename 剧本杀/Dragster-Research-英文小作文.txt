My CO2 Dragster Car Research

For our Design and Technology project, I am making a CO2 dragster car. Before building it, I researched aerodynamics.

Aerodynamics is about how air moves around an object. When a car moves, air pushes against it. This is called drag. More drag means a slower car.

From my research, I learned that a fast dragster should be light, smooth, and streamlined. A pointed front lets air flow over the car easily, while a flat front creates more drag. A lighter car accelerates faster, so I will sand the body until it is smooth. The wheels must be straight too.

In conclusion, my design will have a pointed nose, a narrow tail, and smooth surfaces. I hope my dragster wins the race!
