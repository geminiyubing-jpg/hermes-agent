PART 1: WHY DO I KEEP OPENING MY EYES?
------------------------------------------------
Your brain has a tiny "security guard" inside.
When it gets nervous, it keeps checking the room
every few seconds — open eyes... close... open...

The problem: every time you open your eyes and
see darkness, the guard gets MORE nervous, and
wants to check again. It's a loop!

The fix: give the guard a day off. Here's how.


PART 2: THE 4 MAGIC TRICKS 🪄
------------------------------------------------

TRICK 1: WEAR AN EYE MASK 🥇 (Best one!)
- With a mask on, opening your eyes is useless
- The guard realizes: "Nothing to check here!"
- The loop breaks, your body finally relaxes
- Might feel weird for 2-3 nights, then it's magic
- No mask? Gently lay a soft T-shirt over your eyes

TRICK 2: SLEEP FACING THE WALL 🥈
- Sleep on your side, face toward the wall
- Open your eyes? All you see is... wall. Boring!
- The guard gets bored and goes home

TRICK 3: DISTRACT YOUR EARS 🥉
- Play soft music or an audio story (volume LOW)
- Set a 30-minute timer so it stops by itself
- A brain busy listening forgets to open eyes

TRICK 4: TRY TO STAY AWAKE! 🏅 (Weird but works)
- Tell yourself: "I will NOT sleep tonight!"
- Keep your eyes OPEN and try hard to stay awake
- Result: eyes get tired... brain gets bored...
- ZZZZ. You fall asleep by accident!
- Scientists call this "paradoxical intention"


PART 3: BONUS — THE BURRITO WRAP 🌯
------------------------------------------------
- Wrap your blanket snugly around you
- Being "hugged" by your blanket = feeling safe
- A safe feeling = the guard takes a nap too


PART 4: IF YOU WAKE UP AT 1-3 AM 🌙
------------------------------------------------
Step 1: DO NOT check the clock or your phone
        (Knowing the time only makes you stressed)

Step 2: Remember this secret:
        "Lying still IS resting. My body is
         recharging even if I'm not asleep."
        (This is scientifically true!)

Step 3: Breathe slowly — in 4 seconds,
        out 6 seconds. Do it 5 times.

Step 4: Give your brain a boring job:
        - Count an endless staircase (1, 2, 3...)
        - Recite times tables slowly (1x1, 1x2...)
        - Scan your body: toes relax, feet relax...

Step 5: Still scared? Hug your Jellycat tight 🧸
        and remember: your room in the dark is
        EXACTLY the same room as in the day.
        Same books. Same toys. Same everything.
        Nothing new appears in the dark. Promise.


PART 5: THE FEAR IS A TRICK 🧠
------------------------------------------------
Your brain sometimes makes up scary stuff when
it can't see clearly. But imagination is not real!

The dark corner you're worried about?
There's only a chair and your backpack there.
Your brain is just telling stories — and you
don't have to believe every story it tells.

FINAL WORDS FROM KABI 💛
------------------------------------------------
Being afraid of the dark doesn't mean you're
brave-less. It means your alarm system is
sensitive — which also means you notice things
others miss. That's a superpower, you just need
to learn to turn the volume down.

Try the eye mask tonight. Give it a week.
You've got this! 🌟
