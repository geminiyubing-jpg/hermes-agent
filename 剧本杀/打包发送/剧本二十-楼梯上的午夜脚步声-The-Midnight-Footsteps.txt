═══════════════════════════════════════════════════
   THE MIDNIGHT FOOTSTEPS ON THE STAIRS 👣
   (A 2-Player Spooky-Comedy Mystery Script)
   
   楼梯上的午夜脚步声 —— 英文两人微恐+搞笑剧本杀
   Spooky level: 🌑🌑🌑🌑 (very creepy buildup,
   gloriously silly truth)
   Age: 10+ | Players: 2 | Time: 40-60 min
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

📖 BACKGROUND (Read Aloud Together)

The dorm building at Old Castle Boarding
School has 101 steps between the ground
floor and the top floor.

For the past EIGHT NIGHTS, at exactly
1:13 am, students on floors 2, 3, and 4
have been woken by the same thing:

FOOTSTEPS. 👣

Going UP. Slow. Heavy. Rhythmic.
Step... step... step...

Always starting at step 1 at 1:13 am.
Always stopping — mid-staircase — at
step 57.

Never step 58. Never step 56. Step 57.
Then nothing. No footsteps coming down.
No door opening. Nothing. The footsteps
climb to step 57 and CEASE TO EXIST. 😨

The school's WhatsApp group has theories:
- "It's the Ghost of the Missing Step"
  (there is no missing step)
- "It's the 1902 Headmaster" (he died in
  1902? nobody can find proof he existed)
- "It's Barry from Year 8 sleepwalking"
  (Barry's roommate confirms Barry sleeps
  "like a parked car")

Last night it got worse: at 1:13 am,
the footsteps came — and this time, a
VOICE. Low, groaning, from step 57:

  "Almost... there... so... heavy...
   why... won't... it... roll..."

Then silence. Then — this is the part
that made three students request room
changes — a strange, distant sound,
like something being DRAGGED. Then
nothing until morning.

The headteacher has given you two —
the Dorm Detectives (self-appointed,
matching notebooks, extremely serious)
— until tonight's 1:13 am to solve it.

You'll be waiting on the staircase.
With a flashlight. And snacks.
(Emotional support snacks. Very
important.) 👣🔦🍪

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🎮 HOW TO PLAY

1. Each player secretly reads ONLY their
   own character card. NO PEEKING! 🙈
2. Play 3 rounds (follow the Round Cards).
3. You may LIE about anything EXCEPT the
   two things marked 【CANNOT LIE】.
4. Final round: accuse each other!
5. Then read THE TRUTH together. 🎉

════════════════════════════════════ sound═════════

🅰️ CHARACTER CARD A — ROBIN (The Insomniac Who Hears Everything)

You are ROBIN. You have ears like a bat
and sleep like a security guard. You were
the FIRST to hear the footsteps, on
night one. You have data:

Night 1: 19 steps heard before silence.
Night 2: 21 steps.
Night 3: 24 steps.
Night 4: 24 steps.
Night 5: 29 steps.
Night 6: 33 steps.
Night 7: 41 steps.
Night 8 (last night): 57 steps + THE VOICE.

The footsteps are getting FURTHER every
night. Toward... something.

YOUR SECRET 🤫:
On night 6, you didn't just HEAR the
footsteps — you LOOKED. You crept to
your door, opened it a crack, and saw,
in the dark, at the turning of the
staircase...

A FIGURE. Tall. Bulky. Carrying something
HUGE and ROUND over its shoulders. It
stopped. It turned its head — slowly —
toward your door...

You slammed the door, dived into bed,
and hid under your blanket until 4 am.
You told everyone you "slept through
everything, haha."

You've also — small detail — been
sneaking extra desserts from the dining
hall at dinner. Two puddings a night,
every night, for a week, hidden in your
room for "stakeout energy." The dining
hall pudding inventory has been mysteri-
ously short all week and the chef, Chef
Bianca, is FURIOUS, posting daily notices:
"TO WHOEVER IS TAKING THE PUDDINGS: I KNOW
IT'S A STUDENT. THE CAMERAS KNOW. TURN
YOURSELF IN."

If anyone connects you to the missing
puddings, you're dead. socially dead.

【CANNOT LIE #1】You SAW a figure on
night 6 carrying something big and round.

【CANNOT LIE #2】You have been taking
extra desserts all week.

YOUR TASKS (1 point each):
□ Hide the pudding situation (if Sage
  guesses it, +2 to Sage)
□ Describe the figure you saw — shape,
  size, sound
□ Find out why Sage has been "sleeping
  in the art room" all week
□ Figure out what "why won't it roll"
  means
□ Bonus +3: figure out the FULL truth

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🅱️ CHARACTER CARD B — SAGE (The Artist With Night Vision)

You are SAGE. You are the school's best
artist and its worst sleeper. You've been
sneaking out of your dorm room every
night this week to sleep in the ART ROOM
(top floor, quiet, smells like paint,
has a couch nobody uses).

From the art room, you can see the top
of the staircase. You've watched the
footsteps mystery from the best seat in
the house.

YOUR SECRET 🤫:
You know EXACTLY what the figure is —
because on night 7, at 1:15 am, it
ARRIVED AT THE ART ROOM DOOR.

It was FINN. The Year 11 kid. Carrying
a giant ball of TAPED-TOGETHER CARDBOARD
over his shoulders like a boulder-carrier
from ancient times. Sweating. Panting.
He didn't see you (you were behind the
couch, frozen, holding a paintbrush like
a sword).

He put the cardboard ball down, whispered
"phase... three... complete," took a
break for four minutes while eating a
pudding (A PUDDING — one of the missing
ones, with Chef Bianca's chocolate drizzle
on it), and then went back down.

The next morning, you checked: the
cardboard ball is still in the art room,
hidden under a painting tarp. It's the
size of a mini fridge. You poked it.
It's hollow. You shook it. Something
inside went "clonk."

You haven't told anyone because: (1) Finn
is twice your size, (2) you're technically
not supposed to be sleeping in the art
room every night — you'd have to confess
to THAT too.

【CANNOT LIE #1】You have been sleeping
in the art room all week.

【CANNOT LIE #2】You saw who it was, and
they carried a giant cardboard ball and
ate a pudding at 1:15 am.

YOUR TASKS (1 point each):
□ Hide your art-room sleeping arrangement
□ Reveal what you saw without sounding
  insane
□ Find out about Robin's dessert inventory
□ Get Robin to admit what they saw on
  night 6 (the FULL description)
□ Bonus +3: figure out the FULL truth
  (what's inside the ball? why step 57?)

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🔁 ROUND 1 — THE INTERROGATION (10 min)

Ask each other:
1. Night 6, 1:14 am. Where were YOU?
2. Robin, you claim you "slept through
   everything." Then how do you have a
   STEP COUNT SPREADSHEET? 🤨
3. Sage, why do you smell like paint
   thinner and couch?

💡 CLUE CARD 1 (read aloud):
"Chef Bianca's pudding ledger, day 8:
'SIX puddings missing this week. SIX.
The security cameras are being checked
tomorrow. I have also noticed the flour
storage is down. And the cardboard tubes
from the art room. And a roll of duct
tape from maintenance. If someone is
BUILDING something with my flour bags
and hiding it somewhere, I will find
them. This is not my first pudding
war.' 😤"

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🔁 ROUND 2 — THE EVIDENCE (10 min)

💡 CLUE CARD 2 (read aloud):
"At 1:13 am last night, the 'voice' from
step 57 said: 'Almost there... so heavy...
why won't it... ROLL.'

New evidence from the maintenance log:
a WHEEL — one wheel, shopping-cart sized,
with duct tape on it — was signed out
from the supply room last week. The
signature says 'ART DEPT.' The art
department claims they signed out 'NO
SUCH WHEEL.' The wheel is MISSING.

Also: step 57 has a fresh DUCT TAPE mark
on the railing, exactly at knee height,
shaped like someone paused there to
rest something heavy. Something they
WANTED to roll. Something that SHOULD
roll. Something with a wheel... that
they couldn't attach yet. 😨"

💡 CLUE CARD 3 (read aloud):
"A note has appeared on the Dorm
Detectives' door (this note is for you):

  'Stop investigating the stairs.
   You're getting close to something
   you don't understand.
   The 1:13 truth is bigger than
   you think.
      — A Friend of the Steps'

The handwriting is large and shaky.
Written with a paintbrush, by the look
of it. And it smells faintly of...
chocolate pudding? 🍮"

Ask each other:
1. Who is "A Friend of the Steps"?!
2. What needs a WHEEL but doesn't
   have one yet?
3. Robin. Sage. One of you ate evidence.
   Which one of you was it? 👀

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🔁 ROUND 3 — FINAL ACCUSATION (5 min)

Each player declares:
"I accuse you of being the Step 57 Ghost
because [reasons]! And I ALSO think you
secretly [guess their secret]!"

Reveal:
- Guessed each other's secrets? (+2 each)
- Figured out the FULL truth (Finn, the
  ball, the wheel, the performance)? (+3)

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🎉 THE TRUTH (Read Aloud Together)

1. The "ghost" is FINN, Year 11 — and the
   footsteps, the voice, and the dragging
   are all part of his SECRET PROJECT:

   THE WORLD'S LARGEST CARDBOARD HAMSTER
   WHEEL. 🐹⚙️

   For the school's Art & Engineering
   Fair. Human-sized. Climb-inside-able.
   He's been building it in pieces in
   the art room at night (top floor) and
   carrying each section up the stairs
   at 1:13 am — because that's when the
   night guard change happens and nobody
   watches the staircase.

2. Why 1:13 am EXACTLY? Finn set three
   phone alarms labeled "GUARD CHANGE,"
   "GO NOW," and "BE THE HAMSTER."

3. The growing step count: each night he
   carried a BIGGER section further.
   Night 8 was the FINAL, LARGEST piece —
   the main wheel ring, with the duct-
   taped shopping-cart wheel taped
   inside, rolling it up edge by edge.
   The "voice" ("why won't it roll")
   was Finn talking to the wheel.
   Encouraging it. The wheel did not
   respond. Wheels rarely do.

4. Step 57: that's where the staircase
   TURNS. The wheel-ring was too wide
   to navigate the corner while carrying
   it, so Finn stopped there each night
   to rotate it end-over-end, panting,
   whispering to it. Students one floor
   down heard a giant whispering about
   rolling. For eight nights. Of course
   they thought ghost.

5. The puddings: FINN took the first
   two (fuel for the project — "carbo-
   loading," he called it). Robin, on
   stakeout duty, took four more. Chef
   Bianca's cameras will reveal TWO
   pudding thieves, not one. Robin has
   four days of glory left. Use them
   wisely, Robin.

6. "A Friend of the Steps" — that was
   FINN, trying to scare you off with a
   threatening note. Written with a
   paintbrush (borrowed from Sage's
   art kit — sorry, Sage) at 2 am, on
   paper resting on... a pudding he was
   eating at the time. Hence the smell.
   The world's least anonymous
   anonymous note.

7. Tonight at 1:13 am, you two waited
   on the staircase. Finn arrived,
   carrying the last piece. Flashlight.
   Screaming (all three of you). Then
   explanations. Then — because the
   fair was in two days and he was
   desperate — Finn hired you both as
   official Project Wheel assistants.

8. The Art & Engineering Fair: the
   World's Largest Cardboard Hamster
   Wheel worked. It held the headteacher
   (90 seconds of glorious human hamster
   running before structural integrity
   became a suggestion). Finn won first
   prize. Chef Bianca got a special
   "Pudding Sponsor" credit on the
   plaque and cried (she claims it was
   the onions; there were no onions).

9. Robin's pudding crimes: revealed by
   the cameras, as promised, four days
   later. Punishment: one week of
   kitchen duty. Silver lining: unlimited
   pudding access. Robin calls it "the
   best sentence of my life." Chef
   Bianca has adopted Robin. Nobody
   planned this. Everyone's happy.

WINNER: The player with more points!
EVERYONE: wins, because the ghost was a
Year 11 kid talking to a wheel for eight
nights. 👣🐹⚙️😂

📚 GLOSSARY 词汇表

- footstep = 脚步声 👣
- staircase = 楼梯
- insomniac = 失眠者（Robin的耳机式睡眠 🦇）
- stakeout = 蹲守/蹲点（侦探行为！）
- evidence = 证据
- anonymous note = 匿名纸条（全世界最不匿名的那个）
- hamster wheel = 仓鼠轮 🐹
- structural integrity = 结构完整性（90秒后失效的那种）
- carbo-loading = 碳水加载（Finn偷布丁的理由）
- pudding war = 布丁战争（Bianca主厨的怒火 🍮）
