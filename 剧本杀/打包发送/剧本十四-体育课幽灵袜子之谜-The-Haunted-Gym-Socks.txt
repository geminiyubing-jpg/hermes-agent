═══════════════════════════════════════════════════
   THE CASE OF THE HAUNTED GYM SOCKS 👟
   (A 2-Player Comedy Mystery Script)
   
   体育课幽灵袜子之谜 —— 英文两人搞笑剧本杀
   Age: 10+ | Players: 2 | Time: 40-60 min
   Zero real ghosts. One imaginary one. 100% silly.
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

📖 BACKGROUND (Read Aloud Together)

Welcome to Bramblewood Primary's GYM.

Legend says: if you leave your socks in the
Lost & Found box for 100 days, the SOCK SPIRIT
comes and takes them away forever. 👻

...Okay, that legend was invented by Coach
Dingle last year because he was tired of the
Lost & Found box overflowing. It worked! Kids
started taking their stuff home. Genius.

BUT THIS WEEK, something weird is happening:

Every morning, Coach Dingle's LUCKY SOCKS —
the ones he's worn for every championship
game since 2009 (washed occasionally, we hope)
— keep DISAPPEARING from his office. And
reappearing... in STRANGE PLACES.

Monday: inside the basketball hoop net.
Tuesday: frozen into a ice block in the
        cafeteria freezer.
Wednesday: on the head of the statue of
        the school founder (a man who looks
        confused about everything).

The whole school is now CONVINCED the sock
spirit is real. Kids are scared. Two students
have started leaving "sock offerings" (clean
socks, obviously) by the gym door.

Coach Dingle is losing his mind. Without his
lucky socks, the team has LOST two games in
a row. Tomorrow is the FINAL.

You two are the school's official Mystery
Solvers (self-appointed, badges made of paper).
Find the socks. Save the final. Expose the
"spirit." 🕵️👟

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🎮 HOW TO PLAY

1. Each player secretly reads ONLY their own
   character card. NO PEEKING! 🙈
2. Play 3 rounds (follow the Round Cards).
3. You may LIE about anything EXCEPT the two
   things marked 【CANNOT LIE】.
4. Final round: accuse each other!
5. Then read THE TRUTH together. 🎉

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🅰️ CHARACTER CARD A — ALEX (The Superstitious Athlete)

You are ALEX, star player of the school team.
You take luck VERY seriously:

- You have a pre-game ritual involving 3
  jumping jacks, a spinach smoothie, and
  whispering "be the ball" to the ball.
- You've never walked under a ladder. You've
  crossed the street to avoid one.
- When the "sock spirit" rumor started, you
  PANICKED — but then you realized something:

YOUR SECRET 🤫:
YOUR LUCKY SHOELACES went missing last week!
You believe the sock spirit took them and
will curse the team before the final. So
you've been secretly trying to APPEASE the
spirit — you're the one leaving "sock
offerings" by the gym door (clean socks,
bought with your pocket money in bulk,
the store clerk now thinks you have
"a sock situation").

If anyone finds out you bought 6 pairs of
socks to bribe a ghost, you will never live
it down.

ALSO — and this is the dangerous one —
yesterday at 5 pm you were seen coming out
of Coach's office looking "shifty." Why?
You were checking if Coach's office had
"spirit energy" (you have an app for that;
the app is a flashlight with a green filter).
You didn't take anything!

【CANNOT LIE #1】You HAVE been leaving sock
offerings at the gym door.

【CANNOT LIE #2】You did NOT take Coach's
lucky socks from his office.

YOUR TASKS (1 point each):
□ Hide your sock-offering secret
□ Convince Sam you're not the sock thief
□ Find out why Sam knows so much about
  the cafeteria freezer schedule
□ Discover what Sam was doing at school
  at 6:45 am on Tuesday
□ Bonus +3: figure out the FULL truth

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🅱️ CHARACTER CARD B — SAM (The Skeptic Scientist)

You are SAM. You believe in FACTS and
EXPERIMENTS. Ghosts? Not real. Sock spirits?
Definitely not real. You've read 9 books
about how rumors spread.

You're secretly enjoying this whole thing
a LITTLE too much, because...

YOUR SECRET 🤫:
You've been running a SECRET EXPERIMENT:
"How fast does a fake rumor spread through
a primary school?" You may have... slightly...
fueled the sock spirit rumor by adding
"eyewitness accounts" to the class group
chat ("I heard moaning from the gym at
night!" — you heard the gym's old pipes
squeaking; close enough).

You feel a LITTLE bad. The experiment was
supposed to be harmless! But now the team
is losing and kids are scared and Coach
Dingle aged approximately 10 years this week.

WORSE: You were in the cafeteria at 6:45 am
on Tuesday (before anyone arrives) —
"repositioning an experiment material" —
and you DEFINITELY saw who put the socks
in the freezer. But if you reveal it, you
must also reveal WHY you were there, and
your experiment gets exposed!

【CANNOT LIE #1】You DID add fake eyewitness
accounts to the group chat.

【CANNOT LIE #2】You DID see who put the
socks in the freezer on Tuesday morning.

YOUR TASKS (1 point each):
□ Hide your rumor experiment (if Alex
  guesses it, +2 to Alex)
□ Convince Alex there's no such thing as
  a sock spirit (impossible mission)
□ Find out about Alex's "sock situation"
□ Get Alex to reveal their pre-game ritual
  (it's hilarious, you've heard rumors)
□ Bonus +3: figure out the FULL truth

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🔁 ROUND 1 — THE INTERROGATION (10 min)

Ask each other:
1. Where were you Monday after school, when
   the socks appeared in the basketball net?
2. Alex: explain the socks by the gym door.
3. Sam: explain why you "definitely don't
   believe in ghosts" but know the exact
   decibel level of the gym pipes. 🤨

💡 CLUE CARD 1 (read aloud):
"The basketball hoop socks appeared Monday
6:50 am — but the gym was LOCKED from Sunday
night. Whoever did it either has a key...
or was already inside... or used the window
that never locks properly (the one Coach
Dingle has reported 11 times)."

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🔁 ROUND 2 — THE EVIDENCE (10 min)

💡 CLUE CARD 2 (read aloud):
"The ice block from Tuesday contains the
socks AND a cafeteria name tag frozen inside:
it reads 'KITCHEN INTERNSHIP — D. FIZZWICK.'
D. Fizzwick is... a Year 6 student. Who has
kitchen internship duty on Tuesdays.
And who was recently CUT from the school
team. 👀"

💡 CLUE CARD 3 (read aloud):
"Wednesday, the statue socks: the statue's
head is 3 meters high. You'd need a ladder
from the supply closet... The supply closet
log shows the ladder signed out by 'COACH
DINGLE' on Wednesday morning. But Coach
Dingle was at a dentist appointment on
Wednesday morning. Someone signed his name.
The signature, on close inspection, spells
'COACH DINGEL.' Extra E. Classic forgery."

Ask each other:
1. Who is D. Fizzwick and what's their motive?
2. Why would someone frame Coach Dingle with
   a bad forgery?
3. Alex, seriously, what's in your backpack
   that keeps... squeaking? 🤨

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🔁 ROUND 3 — FINAL ACCUSATION (5 min)

Each player declares:
"I accuse you of the sock crimes because
[reasons]! And I ALSO think you secretly
[guess their secret]!"

Reveal:
- Guessed each other's secrets? (+2 each)
- Figured out the FULL truth (D. Fizzwick's
  identity AND the frozen name tag AND the
  forgery)? (+3)

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🎉 THE TRUTH (Read Aloud Together)

1. The "sock spirit" has a name: D. FIZZWICK,
   Year 6, recently CUT from the team during
   tryouts. Fizzwick wanted Coach's lucky
   socks gone so the team would LOSE and
   everyone would see "lucky socks are
   nonsense." Ironically, Fizzwick's plan
   made everyone believe in the socks MORE.

2. Monday: entered through the broken window,
   socks into the hoop net. (Fizzwick is
   also on the gymnastics team; climbing is
   their specialty.)

3. Tuesday: as kitchen intern, Fizzwick had
   freezer access at 6:45 am. Froze the
   socks. Dropped their name tag in the ice
   by accident — the only mistake. Sam SAW
   Fizzwick doing it but stayed quiet to
   protect the experiment. (Sam feels bad.

   Really bad. Sam has written an apology
   letter and a 3-page " Rumors: A Study "
   report anyway.)

4. Wednesday: the statue! Fizzwick borrowed
   the ladder, signed "COACH DINGEL" (hand
   was shaking from nerves), put the socks
   on the founder's head like a tiny hat.

5. At the final game: you two confronted
   Fizzwick with the evidence. Fizzwick
   cracked in 40 seconds and confessed
   everything, including the part where they
   kept the socks in their pencil case
   between crimes (the whole pencil case now
   smells like destiny and feet).

6. Coach Dingle got his socks back. Team
   WON the final 3-1. Coach cried. Everyone
   pretended not to see. Fizzwick got
   cafeteria duty for a month — where they
   must now guard the freezer with honor.

7. Alex's lucky shoelaces? They were under
   Alex's bed the WHOLE TIME. The sock
   offerings stopped. The store clerk misses
   Alex's business.

8. Sam published the rumor study in the
   school paper. Title: "GHOSTS: IT'S
   ALWAYS PIPES AND GRUDGE-HOLDING
   YEAR 6 STUDENTS." It won a prize. 🏆

WINNER: The player with more points!
EVERYONE: wins, because there was never a
ghost — just pipes, ice, and one dramatic
Year 6 villain. 👟✨

📚 GLOSSARY 词汇表

- superstition / superstitious = 迷信
- lucky socks = 幸运袜
- offering = 供品（Alex拿袜子贿赂"鬼"😂）
- skeptic = 怀疑论者（Sam：只信科学）
- rumor = 谣言
- eyewitness account = 目击证词
- forgery = 伪造签名
- internship = 实习
- cut from the team = 被球队淘汰
- destiny and feet = 命运与脚丫子的味道 👟
