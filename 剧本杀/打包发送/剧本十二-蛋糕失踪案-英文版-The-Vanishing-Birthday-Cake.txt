   THE CASE OF THE VANISHING BIRTHDAY CAKE 🎂
   (A 2-Player Comedy Mystery Script)
   
   蛋糕失踪案 —— 英文两人搞笑剧本杀
   Age: 10+ | Players: 2 | Time: 40-60 min
   Zero ghosts. Zero scary stuff. 100% silly.
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

📖 BACKGROUND (Read Aloud Together 公共背景)

Today is TAYLOR's birthday! 🎉

Taylor's grandma baked a legendary THREE-TIER
CHOCOLATE FUDGE CAKE. It took her 6 hours.
It weighs more than the class hamster (RIP
Sir Fluffington III, he weighed a lot).

The cake was placed in the staff room fridge
at 12:00 pm. Party starts at 3:00 pm.

At 2:45 pm... THE CAKE IS GONE. 😱

The fridge is EMPTY (well, except for Mr. Pickles'
sad lettuce and a yogurt nobody claims).

You two are BEST FRIENDS. But let's be honest —
when a 6-hour grandma cake disappears, even best
friends become detectives. 🔍

One of you MIGHT be the cake thief.
Or maybe not.
Or MAYBE this is all one giant misunderstanding.

Find the truth. Save the party.
And may the crumbs be ever in your favor. 🕵️

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🎮 HOW TO PLAY (玩法说明)

1. Each player secretly reads ONLY their own
   character card. NO PEEKING! 🙈
2. Play 3 rounds (follow the Round Cards).
3. You may LIE about anything EXCEPT the two
   things marked 【CANNOT LIE】.
4. At the end, each player accuses the other.
   The most accurate detective wins!
5. Then read THE TRUTH together. 🎉

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🅰️ CHARACTER CARD A — TAYLOR (The Birthday Kid)

You are TAYLOR. Today is YOUR birthday.
Your grandma's 6-hour cake is GONE and if you
don't find it, your party will just be...
a sad gathering of juice boxes. 😭

WHAT EVERYONE KNOWS ABOUT YOU:
- You were in the gym at 2:15 pm (basketball
  practice, witnessed by 20 people).
- You love cake more than life itself.

YOUR SECRET 🤫:
Last night, Grandma made a TEST CAKE to practice.
You ate the ENTIRE test cake. Alone. At midnight.
With a fork. In your blanket fort.
If anyone finds out you are a MIDNIGHT CAKE MONSTER,
you will die of embarrassment. 💀(not a real ghost,
just an expression, relax)

【CANNOT LIE #1】You DID eat a whole test cake
last night by yourself.

【CANNOT LIE #2】You did NOT touch the birthday
cake today. (True!)

YOUR TASKS (1 point each):
□ Convince Jordan you are NOT the cake thief
□ Hide the fact that you ate a whole test cake
  last night (if Jordan guesses it, they get +2)
□ Find out why Jordan has chocolate on their sleeve
□ Discover where Jordan was at 2:00 pm sharp
□ Bonus +3: figure out the FULL truth of what
  happened to the cake

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🅱️ CHARACTER CARD B — JORDAN (The Best Friend / Prime Suspect)

You are JORDAN, Taylor's best friend since
Year 2. You are also, unfortunately, the
PRIME SUSPECT. Why? Because:

EXHIBIT A: There is CHOCOLATE on your sleeve. 🍫
EXHIBIT B: Sam saw you "sneaking" near the staff
room at 2:00 pm. 👀

But you are INNOCENT. Mostly. Sort of. Okay,
you did ONE sneaky thing, but it wasn't stealing!

YOUR SECRET 🤫:
You were near the fridge because you were trying
to hide a SURPRISE inside: a birthday card with
a glitter bomb that explodes when opened. 💥✨
You got the idea from the internet. It cost you
three weeks of pocket money.

BUT the glitter bomb accidentally went off —
IN YOUR FACE. That's why you also have glitter
in your eyebrows right now. You've been telling
people it's "dandruff, but fancy."

The chocolate on your sleeve? Completely
unrelated: you face-planted into a chocolate
pudding at lunch. It was traumatic. The pudding
never saw it coming.

【CANNOT LIE #1】You DID go to the staff room
at 2:00 pm.

【CANNOT LIE #2】You did NOT take or eat the cake.

YOUR TASKS (1 point each):
□ Convince Taylor you are NOT the cake thief
□ Hide the glitter bomb failure (if Taylor
  guesses it, they get +2)
□ Find out what Taylor did last night at midnight
□ Make Taylor admit whether they could eat an
  entire cake alone (for science)
□ Bonus +3: figure out the FULL truth of what
  happened to the cake

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🔁 ROUND 1 — THE INTERROGATION (10 min)

Player A goes first. Ask each other:
1. Where were you at 2:00 pm? EXACTLY.
2. Explain the chocolate/food stains on you.
3. On a scale of 1-10, how much do you love cake?

💡 CLUE CARD 1 (read aloud):
"The staff room door was UNLOCKED at 2:00 pm.
The fridge was making a weird noise...
like *bzzzt... click... bzzzt...* 🤔"

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🔁 ROUND 2 — THE EVIDENCE (10 min)

💡 CLUE CARD 2 (read aloud):
"Found near the fridge: a tiny paper circle,
a trail of glitter, and one (1) sad lettuce
leaf that clearly witnessed everything."

💡 CLUE CARD 3 (read aloud):
"Mrs. Nimbus, the librarian, says she saw
'Mr. Pickles carrying a large box toward the
library at 2:30 pm, walking suspiciously fast
for a man who usually walks like a sleepy turtle.'"

Ask each other:
1. Explain the glitter. (BOTH of you, glitter
   suspects are everywhere.)
2. What would YOU have done if you stole the
   cake? Describe your perfect cake crime.
   (This is a trap question. Have fun with it.)

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🔁 ROUND 3 — FINAL ACCUSATION (5 min)

Each player declares:
"I accuse you of stealing the cake because
[reasons]! And I ALSO think you secretly
[guess their secret]!"

Then reveal:
- Did you guess each other's secrets? (+2 each)
- Did either of you figure out the FULL truth? (+3)

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🎉 THE TRUTH (Read Aloud Together 真相)

NOBODY stole the cake. Here's what happened:

1. At 2:00 pm, Jordan sneaked in to plant the
   glitter bomb card. The fridge was making a
   weird noise — because the fridge was BREAKING.
   Jordan heard footsteps, panicked, grabbed the
   card, glitter bomb exploded in face, escaped
   with glitter eyebrows. The cake was still there.

2. At 2:30 pm, Mr. Pickles noticed the fridge
   was dying (RIP fridge, 2013-2026 🪦). He
   rescued the cake, carried it to the library
   in a box (waddling fast like a penguin with
   somewhere to be), and asked Mrs. Nimbus to
   guard it with her life.

3. Why didn't he tell anyone? He was ALSO hiding
   something: earlier that day, he secretly ate
   the strawberry on top. His exact words to
   Mrs. Nimbus: "I had to quality-test it.
   For safety. It passed. Delicious."

4. At 3:00 pm, Mr. Pickles walked into the party
   with the cake, one strawberry short, and
   everyone screamed with joy. Taylor blew out
   candles. Jordan's eyebrows sparkled in the
   birthday photos. Forever. ✨

5. Taylor's test cake secret stayed safe...
   UNTIL Grandma asked why Taylor didn't seem
   hungry at the party. Everyone looked at Taylor.
   Taylor looked at the ceiling. Case closed. 😂

WINNER: The player with more points!
EVERYONE ELSE: also winners, because cake. 🎂

📚 GLOSSARY 词汇表 (for international school pros)

- vanish / vanish-ing = 消失 (disappear)
- suspect = 嫌疑人
- prime suspect = 头号嫌疑人
- evidence / exhibit = 证据 / 证物
- accuse / accusation = 指控
- interrogation = 审问
- sneak / sneaked = 偷偷溜
- alibi = 不在场证明 (where you were)
- red herring = 烟雾弹/误导线索
- dandruff, but fancy = 高级头皮屑 😂
- quality-test = 质量检测 (Mr. Pickles' excuse)
