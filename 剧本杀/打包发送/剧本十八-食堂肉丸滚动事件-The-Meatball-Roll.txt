═══════════════════════════════════════════════════
   THE CASE OF THE LUNCHROOM MEATBALL ROLL 🍝
   (A 2-Player Comedy Mystery Script)
   
   食堂肉丸滚动事件 —— 英文两人搞笑剧本杀
   Age: 10+ | Players: 2 | Time: 40-60 min
   Zero ghosts. 100% cafeteria chaos.
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

📖 BACKGROUND (Read Aloud Together)

It was 12:47 pm on Meatball Wednesday.
The most sacred day of the school week.

The cafeteria was at maximum capacity.
The meatballs were, per tradition,
"perfectly round, suspiciously bouncy,
and 40% mystery" — the exact recipe that
made them school legends.

And then it happened.

A SINGLE meatball — launched from
somewhere near Table 5 — rolled the
ENTIRE length of the cafeteria floor.
Past Table 4. Past Table 3. Down the
little ramp by the recycling bins.
Through the doors (someone had left
them open — thanks, Kevin).

It rolled 47 METERS in total — through
the hallway, past the trophy case,
around the corner (somehow taking the
turn like a professional), and came to
rest... inside Mr. Wobbleknees' shoe.

His shoe. While he was wearing it.

The school has watched the security
footage 400 times. The meatball's
journey has been set to epic music.
Someone made a documentary. The
documentary has a sequel in production.

But ONE question remains unanswered:

WHO LAUNCHED THE MEATBALL? 🕵️

The security camera only caught the
meatball from behind (the camera angle
is, famously, "the ceiling"). The launch
itself is a mystery.

The two of you were sitting at Table 5.
You are the only witnesses. And possibly
the culprits.

The principal says no Meatball Wednesday
EVER AGAIN until someone confesses.

This is war. Find the launcher. 🍝

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🎮 HOW TO PLAY

1. Each player secretly reads ONLY their own
   character card. NO PEEKING! 🙈
2. Play 3 rounds (follow the Round Cards).
3. You may LIE about anything EXCEPT the two
   things marked 【CANNOT LIE】.
4. Final round: accuse each other!
5. Then read THE TRUTH together. 🎉

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🅰️ CHARACTER CARD A — NOVA (The Sports Star)

You are NOVA. Captain of literally every
team (netball, football, tag rugby — the
school had to invent a rule: "one captaincy
per student, Nova, please, we beg you").

Your arm is CANNON-FAMOUS. You once threw
a paper airplane from the library window
and it landed in the swimming pool (outdoors,
across the yard, 60 meters, no wind assist).

Everyone assumes YOU launched the meatball,
because who else could roll a meatball 47
meters with such BEAUTIFUL form? Even you
have to admit — the meatball's trajectory
was elegant. Suspiciously elegant.

YOUR SECRET 🤫:
At 12:47 pm, you were NOT eating lunch.
You were under the table, retrieving your
LITERAL LUCKY PEN — a pen with a tiny
football on top, given to you by your
grandma, dropped during the excitement
of Meatball Wednesday.

You crawled under the table, and while
down there, you saw SOMETHING from the
best angle in the whole cafeteria: FEET.
You saw exactly whose feet moved at the
moment of launch, and what those feet
were wearing.

The problem: revealing what you saw means
admitting you were crawling under the
cafeteria tables like a gremlin during
the most famous moment in school history.
You have an image to maintain! You're a
CAPTAIN!

Also — your tray at 12:47 pm had a meatball
MISSING. One meatball. You swear you ate
it. Did you eat it? You genuinely can't
remember. It was a chaotic lunch. There's
a small chance your meatball count is off
by one. A SMALL chance.

【CANNOT LIE #1】You were under the table
at 12:47 pm.

【CANNOT LIE #2】You cannot 100% confirm
all your meatballs were accounted for.

YOUR TASKS (1 point each):
□ Maintain your dignity while explaining
  the under-table situation
□ Hide the lucky pen story (if Kai guesses
  it, +2 to Kai)
□ Reveal what the FEET told you — at the
  right moment, for maximum drama
□ Figure out what Kai was filming
□ Bonus +3: figure out the FULL truth

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🅱️ CHARACTER CARD B — KAI (The Filmmaker)

You are KAI. You carry your tablet
everywhere and film EVERYTHING. Your
channel, "KaiSeesAll," has 47 subscribers
(46 of them are your cousin with 46
accounts — long story — and one is a
stranger from Norway who comments "nice"
on everything. You love him).

Meatball Wednesday is your best content
day. You ALWAYS film the meatball tray
arrival ("the unboxing").

YOUR SECRET 🤫:
You were filming when the meatball
launched. YOU HAVE THE LAUNCH ON CAMERA.
The whole thing. The hand. The flick.
The angle. Everything.

This footage is worth EVERYTHING. If you
release it: instant fame, the mystery
solved, Meatball Wednesday restored.

BUT — and here's the dilemma — you've
ALREADY sold the exclusive rights to the
school newspaper's editor, Priya, for
$4 and a juice box (paid in advance,
consumed immediately — you have regrets
on multiple levels). The deal says you
can't show ANYONE until Friday's edition.

Priya is UNREACHABLE until Friday (phone
confiscated — something about a "hallway
scooter incident," unrelated, she says).
You CANNOT release the footage without
breaking the deal. A journalist's word
is sacred. Even a $4 journalist's.

Also, minor complication: at 12:46 pm,
one minute before the launch, YOUR tray
was momentarily unattended (you were
adjusting the camera angle). You didn't
see anyone touch it. But you also
didn't NOT see anyone touch it. Your
meatball inventory is... unverified.

【CANNOT LIE #1】You have the launch on
camera but cannot show it until Friday.

【CANNOT LIE #2】You left your tray
unattended at 12:46 pm.

YOUR TASKS (1 point each):
□ Keep the footage secret (if Nova figures
  out what's on it, +2 to Nova)
□ Get Nova to explain the under-table
  gremlin era
□ Find out whose shoes Nova saw
□ Decide together: solve it now with logic,
  or wait for Friday's footage?
□ Bonus +3: figure out the FULL truth
  (including the third party's role)

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🔁 ROUND 1 — THE INTERROGATION (10 min)

Ask each other:
1. Where exactly were you at 12:47 pm?
   The ceiling camera says you both
   "vibrated with excitement." Explain.
2. Nova: why were your knees dusty at
   12:50 pm? 🤨
3. Kai: why do you keep hugging your
   tablet like it owes you money?

💡 CLUE CARD 1 (read aloud):
"Forensic analysis of the meatball's path
(the janitor, Mr. Bok, traced the sauce
trail with professional calligraphy
precision): the launch came from the
LEFT side of Table 5. Nova sat on the
left. But ALSO — the launch angle was
LOW. Suspiciously low. Almost like it
came from... under the table??"

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🔁 ROUND 2 — THE EVIDENCE (10 min)

💡 CLUE CARD 2 (read aloud):
"New witness: Kevin (of 'left the doors
open' fame) says at 12:46 pm he saw
'The new kid, Fenn, doing practice
flicks with a grape at the end of
Table 5. Very focused. Like an athlete
warming up. I thought it was weird but
I was busy failing to hold a door.'"

💡 CLUE CARD 3 (read aloud):
"The cafeteria's meatball inventory
(a clipboard that nobody knew existed
until now) shows: Table 5 received 12
meatballs for 4 students. After launch:
11 on trays. 1 on Mr. Wobbleknees'
shoe adventure. Math checks out.
BUT Fenn's tray — at the END of Table 5 —
had TWO meatballs with a single bite
taken out of each. Who bites a meatball
once and moves to the next?! What is
this, a tasting menu?!"

Ask each other:
1. Who is Fenn and why is he flicking
   produce like a pro?
2. Nova — you saw feet. TIME TO SHARE
   THE FEET.
3. Kai — hypothetically, if someone had
   footage, when could said footage be
   legally, ethically, and juicily
   released? 👀

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🔁 ROUND 3 — FINAL ACCUSATION (5 min)

Each player declares:
"I accuse you of the Great Meatball
Launch because [reasons]! And I ALSO
think you secretly [guess their secret]!"

Reveal:
- Guessed each other's secrets? (+2 each)
- Figured out the FULL truth (Fenn,
  the bite marks, the low angle)? (+3)

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🎉 THE TRUTH (Read Aloud Together)

1. The meatball launcher was... FENN.
   The new kid. Quiet. Mysterious.
   Suspiciously good at flicking things.

2. Fenn's story: at his OLD school, they
   had a legendary game called Meatball
   Bowling — flick a meatball, knock over
   the ketchup bottles at the far table.
   He was the two-time champion. When he
   arrived here, he found no Meatball
   Bowling culture. A tragedy. So he was
   TRAINING. The grape was practice.
   The meatball was the real attempt.

3. The single-bite meatballs on his tray:
   Fenn tests each meatball's "bounce
   density" by biting once. Champion
   behavior, honestly. His standards
   are elite.

4. The launch angle: LOW because Fenn
   flicked it from table level with
   champion form. Nova, under the table,
   saw Fenn's limited-edition red-and-
   gold sneakers shift into the classic
   "champion flick stance." She knew
   INSTANTLY. She just didn't want to
   explain how she knew. Gremlin shame
   is real.

5. At 3 pm, you two confronted Fenn
   with everything: the grape flicks
   (Kevin), the bite marks (clipboard),
   the stance (Nova's feet testimony),
   and the footage tease (Kai's
   "I have it all on camera, Fenn.
   ALL OF IT." — a bluff! The deal
   meant he couldn't show it. Fenn
   didn't know that).

   Fenn cracked like a piñata and
   confessed everything. Then he asked,
   with hope in his eyes: "Wait... do
   you guys want to see Meatball
   Bowling? I can teach you. I have
   a whole training program. There
   are levels."

6. The verdict: Fenn apologized to
   Mr. Wobbleknees (who secretly kept
   the meatball — it's on his desk,
   lacquered, a trophy now — the man
   is full of surprises). Meatball
   Wednesday was restored, with ONE
   new official rule: "No flicking
   without the bowling pins set up
   in the GYM, supervised, on the
   last Friday of term."

7. The first official Meatball Bowling
   tournament: 64 participants. Nova
   won (of course she did — the arm
   is a gift). Fenn came second and
   declared it "a passing of the torch
   I did not authorize but accept."

8. Kai's footage ran in Friday's paper:
   "THE LAUNCH: EXCLUSIVE." Circulation
   tripled. Priya got her phone back,
   watched the documentary sequel, and
   immediately greenlit part three.
   The Norwegian commenter wrote "nice."
   Best guy.

WINNER: The player with more points!
EVERYONE: wins, because sports were
invented in a cafeteria that day. 🍝✨

📚 GLOSSARY 词汇表

- meatball = 肉丸
- trajectory = 弹道/轨迹
- unattended = 无人看管的
- forensic analysis = 取证分析
- witness = 目击者
- inventory = 库存清单
- confession = 招供
- piñata = 皮纳塔（一打就碎的糖果罐，这里形容Fenn一问就全招了 😂）
- passing of the torch = 火炬传递（冠军交替）
- mystery meat = 谜之肉（食堂肉的传说属性 🤫）
