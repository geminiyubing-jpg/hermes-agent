═══════════════════════════════════════════════════
   THE LAUGHING STATUE IN THE GARDEN 🗿
   (A 2-Player Spooky-Comedy Mystery Script)
   
   花园里会笑的雕像 —— 英文两人微恐+搞笑剧本杀
   Spooky level: 🌑🌑🌑 (one statue, one
   laugh, zero actual danger)
   Age: 10+ | Players: 2 | Time: 40-60 min
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

📖 BACKGROUND (Read Aloud Together)

In the middle of the school's Memorial
Garden stands a statue. It's been there
for 80 years.

It depicts Sir Reginald Fuzzleworth,
the school's founder — a stern man in
a stone frock coat, holding a stone
book, staring eternally at... nothing
in particular. Generations of students
have walked past him. He has never done
anything. He is a statue. That's the
whole deal.

Until Tuesday.

At 12:29 pm on Tuesday — lunchtime, a
crowd of witnesses — somebody HEARD the
statue LAUGH. 🗿

Not a creak. Not wind. A LAUGH.
A low, rumbling "hm hmm hmm hmmm,"
coming — witnesses swear — from INSIDE
the statue. It lasted four seconds.
Then silence. Sir Reginald's stone face
remained stern. But several students
now claim his stone eyes "looked
happier."

It happened again Wednesday, 12:29 pm.
And Thursday, 12:29 pm. EXACTLY 12:29.
Every time. The whole school now gathers
at 12:28 to watch. Yesterday, 200 kids
saw it live. The laugh was captured on
30 phones. Slow-motion. Zoomed. Analyzed.
One kid made a remix.

Theories are out of control:
- "Sir Reginald is waking up" 😨
- "The garden is built on an ancient
  laughing ground" (there is no such
  thing)
- "It's the ghost of his arch-enemy,
  Baron Von Thistlewood, trapped inside
  since 1946" (Baron Von Thistlewood
  was never a real person... probably)

The headteacher says: "If that statue
laughs one more time, someone is paying
to MOVE it."

Today is Friday. Lunchtime is in one
hour. You two have been chosen (you
volunteered, aggressively) to solve the
Case of the Laughing Statue before Sir
Reginald gets evicted. 🗿🕵️

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🎮 HOW TO PLAY

1. Each player secretly reads ONLY their
   own character card. NO PEEKING! 🙈
2. Play 3 rounds (follow the Round Cards).
3. You may LIE about anything EXCEPT the
   two things marked 【CANNOT LIE】.
4. Final round: accuse each other!
5. Then read THE TRUTH together. 🎉

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🅰️ CHARACTER CARD A — PIP (The Garden Lover)

You are PIP. You spend every lunchtime
in the Memorial Garden. You know every
plant, every bench, every worm (the
worms don't know you, that's a one-way
relationship).

You were THREE FEET from the statue
during the first laugh. Closest witness.
It was TERRIFYING and also... kind of
a nice laugh? Warm? Like a grandpa
watching kittens?

YOUR SECRET 🤫:
The laugh wasn't the first weird thing.
For two weeks BEFORE the laughing started,
you noticed the statue was... WET.

Every morning, Sir Reginald's stone
coat has been damp, in a specific spot:
a puddle-shape on his shoulder, like
something sits there at night. Something
warm. You checked at 6 am twice (you
couldn't sleep, don't judge). Both
times: damp shoulder. Warm. In October.
When nothing should be warm.

You also found — pressed into the mud
behind the statue, facing AWAY from the
garden — a small pile of... crumbs?
Like someone sat there eating sandwiches
at night. Facing the wall. Like a
weirdo. Or a ghost. A sandwich ghost. 👻🥪

You haven't told anyone about the damp
shoulder or the crumbs, because:
"A sandwich ghost ate crumbs behind the
statue and warmed its shoulder" is not
a sentence you can say out loud and
keep friends.

【CANNOT LIE #1】You have seen the damp,
warm patch on the statue's shoulder at
6 am.

【CANNOT LIE #2】You found crumbs behind
the statue, pressed into the mud,
facing the wall.

YOUR TASKS (1 point each):
□ Reveal the damp shoulder evidence
  without sounding insane
□ Find out why Mel knows the exact
  temperature of the statue's book
□ Figure out the 12:29 pattern
□ Discover who "The Night Botanist" is
□ Bonus +3: figure out the FULL truth

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🅱️ CHARACTER CARD B — MEL (The Weather Nerd With Equipment)

You are MEL. You run the school's
Weather Club (membership: you). You have
more sensors than friends, and you love
them equally.

Your kit lives in the garden (best wind
readings). Yesterday, you checked your
data and found something IMPOSSIBLE:

YOUR SECRET 🤫:
At exactly 12:29 pm every day this week,
your INFRARED THERMOMETER — pointed at
the garden for weather reasons — recorded
a temperature spike behind the statue:
18°C to 31°C for four seconds. 31°C!
From NOTHING. Then back to 18°C.

A warm SOMETHING appears behind Sir
Reginald at 12:29, for four seconds,
daily. You have three days of data. The
graph looks like the statue is "having
a hot flash" (your notebook's words).

AND — the deep secret — you have a
SECOND sensor nobody knows about: a
HUMIDITY LOGGER hidden in the garden
shed, part of your secret project to
prove the shed is "a microclimate" (it's
not, but a nerd can dream). The logger
shows: every night at 2:40 am, the
humidity spikes for 30 minutes behind
the statue. Like something BREATHES
there. Every night. For two weeks. 😨

You haven't shared this because the
Weather Club's credibility is... fragile.
"Weather kid claims statue breathes" is
how clubs get disbanded.

【CANNOT LIE #1】You recorded a 31°C
temperature spike behind the statue at
12:29 pm, daily.

【CANNOT LIE #2】Your secret humidity
logger shows nightly 2:40 am humidity
spikes behind the statue.

YOUR TASKS (1 point each):
□ Share the temperature data (your
  precious, precious data)
□ Hide the illegal second sensor (if Pip
  guesses the "microclimate shed" project,
  +2 to Pip)
□ Figure out what's warm AND damp AND
  appears at 12:29
□ Get Pip to admit what they found in
  the mud
□ Bonus +3: figure out the FULL truth

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🔁 ROUND 1 — THE INTERROGATION (10 min)

Ask each other:
1. What do you know about 12:29 pm?
   Compare notes. Numbers. Times. DATA.
2. Pip: you're in the garden every day.
   What have you noticed that you're
   not telling me? 👀
3. Mel: why does your backpack beep?

💡 CLUE CARD 1 (read aloud):
"The groundskeeper, Old Tom, was inter-
viewed. He said: 'That statue? Been here
40 years. Never laughed. BUT —' (long
pause, dramatic, he's enjoying this)
'— someone's been pinching my bench.
My workshop bench. Tools move. My good
sandpaper's gone. A whole roll. And my
LEATHER POLISH. Who steals sandpaper
AND leather polish? A very specific
kind of maniac, that's who.' He then
refused to elaborate and watered a
bench for emotional support."

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🔁 ROUND 2 — THE EVIDENCE (10 min)

💡 CLUE CARD 2 (read aloud):
"The audio analysis (the music teacher,
slowed the laugh down 400%): under the
laugh, there's a VOICE. Faint. Saying —
read this slowly — 'that's... it...
that's... the one... beautiful...'

The laugh isn't random. It's RESPONSE
to something. The statue — or whatever
is behind it — is looking at something
at 12:29 pm and DELIGHTED by it. Every
day. The same thing. What happens at
12:29 pm in this school, every single
day?"

💡 CLUE CARD 3 (read aloud):
"12:29 pm. Checked the schedule:
that is EXACTLY when the Year 1 class
walks through the Memorial Garden on
their way to lunch. Every day. Twenty
five-year-olds, holding a rope, walking
past the statue.

One of them — a tiny kid named Poppy —
waves at the statue every single day.
Not at the garden. Not at friends. At
the STATUE. She was interviewed: 'I wave
at the man. He likes it. I can tell.
He has kind eyes now.'

The camera footage confirms: the laugh
happens during the exact SECOND Poppy's
hand goes up. Every day. 🗿👋"

Ask each other:
1. What is warm, damp, appears at 12:29,
  breathes at 2:40 am, likes sandpaper
  and leather polish... and is delighted
  by a five-year-old waving?
2. Pip. The crumbs. The DAMP WARM
  SHOULDER. Say it. Say the sentence.
3. Mel. If something LIVES behind that
  statue... what size is it? Check your
  data. Check it now. 😨

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🔁 ROUND 3 — FINAL ACCUSATION (5 min)

Each player declares:
"I accuse you of animating the statue
because [reasons]! And I ALSO think you
secretly [guess their secret]!"

Reveal:
- Guessed each other's secrets? (+2 each)
- Figured out the FULL truth (the
  resident, the 12:29 wave, the polish)? (+3)

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🎉 THE TRUTH (Read Aloud Together)

1. Living behind the statue, in a gap
   between the plinth and the garden
   wall, is... a CAT. 🐈

   A very old, very large, extremely
   dignified ginger cat. The school has
   never officially had a cat. He has
   unofficially existed for approximately
   nine years. His name — given by the
   kitchen staff, who feed him — is
   SIR FUZZLEWORTH THE SECOND.

2. The 12:29 laugh: when Poppy waves at
   the statue, the cat — sitting on the
   statue's shoulder gap, sun-warmed —
   PURRS. Loudly. Deeply. A 13-pound
   senior cat's purr, echoing through
   the hollow 80-year-old bronze statue,
   amplified by the stone book's bell
   shape, sounds EXACTLY like a stone
   chuckle: "hm hmm hmm hmmm."
   Sir Reginald laughs because a cat
   on his shoulder is happy a child
   waved. That's it. That's the ghost.

3. The damp warm shoulder patch: where
   the cat sleeps. Every night. He is
   warm (31°C, per Mel's thermometer —
   which was measuring CAT the whole
   time). The 2:40 am humidity spike:
   the cat's evening bath. Behind the
   statue. For exactly 30 minutes.
   Cats are punctual.

4. The crumbs: the cat's midnight snack
   spot. He faces the wall because that's
   where the kitchen's back door draft
   brings the smell of the fish deliveries.
   A connoisseur's seat.

5. The sandpaper and leather polish:
   the cat is OLD and his shelter —
   the gap behind the plinth — has a
   wooden floor. Someone has been
   maintaining it. Sanding the rough
   spots (so the old cat doesn't get
   splinters) and polishing the small
   wooden ledge he sleeps on (leather
   polish = wood polish, in a pinch,
   according to a madman). WHO would
   do this?

   OLD TOM. The groundskeeper. He found
   the cat nine years ago as a kitten,
   stuck in the drain. He never told
   anyone (school rules: no pets). He
   has been secretly feeding it, vet-ing
   it ("my cousin's a vet, don't ask"),
   and renovating its house with stolen
   supplies from his own workshop, for
   NINE YEARS. When he heard the
   "laughing statue" panic, he nearly
   cried. "They're going to move him.
   They'll FIND him. He hates change.
   He's SO old."

6. You two cracked it at 12:20 pm, five
   minutes before the laugh. At 12:29,
   from behind the plinth, you watched
   the Year 1 rope walk by. Poppy waved.
   And from the shoulder gap, an ancient
   ginger face rose slowly... and purred
   like a cement mixer full of bees.
   The crowd heard the laugh. You two
   saw the truth. You kept the secret.

7. The deal you struck with the
   headteacher: "The statue stays. The
   cat stays. We will call him the
   Garden Warden. Official title. He
   requires zero budget, and in exchange
   he will continue being a local
   legend." The headteacher met the cat.
   The cat blinked slowly at her. She
   signed the paperwork on the spot.

8. Sir Fuzzleworth the Second now has a
   tiny bronze plaque: "GARDEN WARDEN.
   EST. 9 YEARS. WAVE AT HIM. HE LIKES
   IT." Poppy waves every day. The purr
   rumbles at 12:29. The school has
   never been happier. Old Tom got a
   budget line for "garden maintenance"
   that everyone knows means "cat food."
   Nobody has ever said it out loud.
   Some truths are too perfect to
   bureaucratize. 🐈🗿✨

WINNER: The player with more points!
EVERYONE: wins, because the ghost was a
senior cat who has been legally employed
as a statue's shoulder for nine years.

📚 GLOSSARY 词汇表

- statue = 雕像 🗿
- purr = 猫的呼噜声
- plinth = 雕像底座
- humidity = 湿度（Mel的传感器 📊）
- infrared thermometer = 红外测温仪
- groundskeeper = 园丁/场地管理员
- microclimate = 微气候（Mel的执念）
- connoisseur = 行家/鉴赏家（面对鱼腥味方向的猫）
- warden = 管理员（猫的官方职位 🐈)
- too perfect to bureaucratize = 完美到不能写进制度 😂
