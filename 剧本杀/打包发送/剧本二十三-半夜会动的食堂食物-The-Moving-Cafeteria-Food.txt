═══════════════════════════════════════════════════
   THE CAFETERIA FOOD THAT MOVES AT NIGHT 🍮
   (A 2-Player Spooky-Comedy Mystery Script)
   
   半夜会动的食堂食物 —— 英文两人微恐+搞笑剧本杀
   Spooky level: 🌑🌑🌑 (creepy CCTV, warm
   truth, zero ghosts)
   Age: 10+ | Players: 2 | Time: 40-60 min
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

📖 BACKGROUND (Read Aloud Together)

Chef Bianca runs the cafeteria with an
iron whisk. Every night she preps the
next day's dessert — her legendary
CARAMEL PUDDINGS — lines them up on the
steel counter, covers them, locks the
kitchen at 8 pm, and goes home.

For the past FIVE mornings, something
has been... OFF. 😨

Monday: the pudding covers were neatly
stacked in a pyramid. Fine. Weird but
fine.

Tuesday: the puddings were all ROTATED
to face the door. All 40 of them.
Uniformly. Like an audience.

Wednesday: two puddings were MISSING.
In their place: a thank-you note, in
tiny neat handwriting: "very good.
thank you. — a friend" The note was
written on a napkin. With what appeared
to be... chocolate sauce?

Thursday: no puddings missing. But a
pudding was ADDED. A homemade one.
Small. Lopsided. Sitting proudly at
the end of the line. Chef Bianca, a
professional of 20 years, examined it
and said, quietly, "it's... better
than mine." She did not take this
well. She has not slept since.

Friday (this morning): the CCTV was
finally checked. The footage from 2 am,
every night, shows the same thing:

A SHADOW — small, quick, low to the
ground — moving between the counters.
Too fast to see clearly. Too small to
be a person. Too BIG to be a mouse.
It moves like it knows the kitchen
intimately. It opens the fridge (the
HANDLE, mind you, not a crack — the
HANDLE), takes nothing, closes it,
touches a few puddings lovingly(?),
and vanishes near the corner vent.

The video has been watched by everyone.
Half the school says "cat." The other
half says "impossible — no cat opens
fridges, that's a PERSON-sized ghost
with PERSON hands doing CAT cos play."

Chef Bianca has given you two — the
Investigators of Weird Stuff — full
kitchen access until 8 pm tonight.
She wants answers. She wants her
pudding honor restored. She wants to
know who made a better pudding in her
own kitchen.

Solve it before the 2 am shadow
returns. Or, better: solve it and BE
HERE at 2 am. 🍮🌙

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🎮 HOW TO PLAY

1. Each player secretly reads ONLY their
   own character card. NO PEEKING! 🙈
2. Play 3 rounds (follow the Round Cards).
3. You may LIE about anything EXCEPT the
   two things marked 【CANNOT LIE】.
4. Final round: accuse each other!
5. Then read THE TRUTH together. 🎉

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🅰️ CHARACTER CARD A — BEA (The Chef's Apprentice)

You are BEA. You idolize Chef Bianca.
You've memorized her recipes. You want
to be a chef when you grow up. You are
also — and this is relevant — the only
student with a KEY to the kitchen
(apprentice privileges; Chef says you
have "the wrists").

You've been keeping a secret from your
best friend Willa, and it's eating you:

YOUR SECRET 🤫:
Three weeks ago, staying late for
apprentice dish duty, you found a
TORN PAGE from an old recipe book
wedged behind the corner vent. It was
handwritten. Beautiful old cursive.
Title: "Miss Akko's Pudding — 1974."

Miss Akko. The cafeteria chef from
1974-1998. The woman who TRAINED Chef
Bianca. The woman whose recipe book
was LOST when she retired (Chef Bianca
has mentioned this with genuine grief
approximately 500 times — "her caramel
was superior, we've been chasing it
for 30 years").

The page had THE caramel pudding
recipe. THE one. You made it. Alone.
At night. Twice. It came out... perfect.
Exactly like the legend says. Better
than Bianca's (don't say that out
loud, Bea, come on).

You left one on the counter Thursday —
wait, no. Let's be honest. You didn't
leave it. You made TWO. Ate one. Left
one. Because if Chef found an unex-
plained perfect pudding, she'd have to
investigate, and the investigation
leads to the vent, and the vent has
THE PAGE, and the page proves you've
been night-cooking in her kitchen
without permission. You were trying to
plant a mystery to cover a mystery.
You are a genius or a disaster. Unclear.

【CANNOT LIE #1】You found Miss Akko's
lost pudding recipe behind the vent.

【CANNOT LIE #2】You made the mystery
pudding Thursday night.

YOUR TASKS (1 point each):
□ Hide your night-cooking (if Willa
  guesses it, +2 to Willa)
□ Figure out who ate two puddings and
  left a chocolate-sauce note
□ Investigate the corner vent properly
□ Decide whether to tell Chef about
  the recipe page — and when
□ Bonus +3: figure out the FULL truth

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🅱️ CHARACTER CARD B — WILLA (The Night Owl With a Secret)

You are WILLA. You know every shadow in
this school because you're always here
early and late — your parents run the
school's front office, so you arrive at
6:30 am and leave at 7 pm. You have a
lot of unsupervised hours. You've filled
them with a hobby:

YOUR SECRET 🤫:
You've been secretly feeding a CAT that
lives near the cafeteria's back door for
two weeks. A big grey one with a torn
ear. He's skittish but clever — you
named him Risotto. He shows up at 7 pm
sharp every evening for the tuna you've
been "losing" from the cafeteria supply
(the ledger has questions; you've been
frame-shifting blame to a hypothetical
"inventory shrinkage").

But here's the thing — Risotto has been
DISAPPEARING at night. You put a small
tracker (your dad's old key finder,
taped to a harness you sewed from a
sock) on him, and the app shows: at 2 am
every night, Risotto enters... the
kitchen. THE KITCHEN WITH THE MOVING
FOOD. Through the corner vent (there's
a gap where the grate is loose —
cat-sized, exactly).

Your cat is the CCTV shadow. Your cat
has been "lovingly touching puddings."
Your cat possibly wrote a chocolate-
sauce note (no — surely no — the
handwriting was TOO NEAT — but WHO
ELSE—).

You haven't told anyone because: (1)
if the school finds a cat in the
kitchen, Risotto's gone, (2) if Chef
Bianca finds out the "tuna inventory
shrinkage" was you, you're done, (3)
you're starting to worry you're the
only sane person in this mystery and
that's a heavy burden.

【CANNOT LIE #1】There is a cat, you've
been feeding it, and it enters the
kitchen via the corner vent at 2 am.

【CANNOT LIE #2】The "missing tuna"
from the cafeteria was taken by you.

YOUR TASKS (1 point each):
□ Protect Risotto at all costs
□ Hide the tuna situation (if Bea
  guesses it, +2 to Bea)
□ Figure out who made the lopsided
  pudding (it wasn't you — you can't
  cook; you once burned cereal)
□ Find out what's behind the vent
  (there's something there beyond
  the cat, isn't there?)
□ Bonus +3: figure out the FULL truth

🔁 ROUND 1 — THE INTERROGATION (10 min)

Ask each other:
1. What do we KNOW about the 2 am
   shadow? Size. Movement. Fridge
   handle. Discuss.
2. Bea: you have a kitchen key. Where
   were you Wednesday night, when two
   puddings vanished and a chocolate
   note appeared?
3. Willa: your backpack meowed on
   Tuesday. Explain. (It meowed. We
   all heard it.) 🎒🐈

💡 CLUE CARD 1 (read aloud):
"Close analysis of the CCTV shadow's
movements: it moves quadrupedally (on
four legs), weighs approximately 4-5
kg (judged by counter pressure), and —
the detail that broke the case open —
has a TORN LEFT EAR visible in one
frame. Also: the chocolate-sauce note.
Handwriting analysis (Ms. Quillfeather,
library, cursive enthusiast): 'This is
the handwriting of an adult. Old-
fashioned loops. Someone who learned
to write BEFORE computers. This is
not a child's hand. This is not a
cat's paw. This is a retired person's
penmanship.' A retired person's
penmanship. In a locked kitchen. At
2 am. 😨"

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🔁 ROUND 2 — THE EVIDENCE (10 min)

💡 CLUE CARD 2 (read aloud):
"The corner vent, examined at last:
the grate lifts away easily. Inside,
beyond the pipe gap: a small cavity.
And in the cavity:

— a cat bed. Homemade. Sewn from what
  appears to be a school woolen sock
  and a dish towel.
— an empty tuna tin, licked spotless
— and a RECIPE BOOK. Old. Water-
  damaged. Handwritten. The cover
  reads: 'Property of Miss Akko —
  DO NOT LOSE (I mean it, Gerald).'

Gerald, upon investigation, was the
caretaker here from 1971-1999. The
book was given to him for safekeeping
when Miss Akko retired. He... put it
in a wall. For 28 years. Gerald is 91
and, reached by phone, said only:
'Oh GOOD, someone found it. I've felt
terrible since 1998. Also tell Bianca
her heat is too high for caramel.
She knows. She's always known.'"

💡 CLUE CARD 3 (read aloud):
"Wait — the cat has been coming and
going through this vent for two weeks.
The book was IN the cavity. But the
torn page ('Miss Akko's Pudding —
1974') that started everything was
found by a STUDENT behind the vent
three weeks ago. The cat's visits and
the page's appearance... happened
within days of each other.

Sequence: cat starts visiting vent
(because a certain someone started
leaving tuna by the back door) → cat
explores cavity → cat knocks loose
the old book → a page slips out into
the kitchen → student finds page →
student makes the legendary pudding →
legendary pudding appears on counter
→ chef has identity crisis → we are
all here. The cat, unknowingly,
unlocked a 28-year-old secret. All
because someone fed it tuna. WHO
FED THE CAT TUNA?!"

Ask each other:
1. WHO. FED. THE CAT. TUNA. (Willa,
   sweat visibly.)
2. Bea — that page. You found it
   three weeks ago. What did you DO
   with the page? 👀
3. What do we tell Chef Bianca? Think
   carefully. Her pudding honor is
   at stake. And Miss Akko's book is
   RIGHT THERE.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🔁 ROUND 3 — FINAL ACCUSATION (5 min)

Each player declares:
"I accuse you of the Kitchen Night
Visits because [reasons]! And I ALSO
think you secretly [guess their
secret]!"

Reveal:
- Guessed each other's secrets? (+2 each)
- Figured out the FULL truth (cat,
  page, book, note, pudding)? (+3)

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

🎉 THE TRUTH (Read Aloud Together)

1. The CCTV shadow: RISOTTO. Willa's
   semi-adopted cat. Vent. 2 am.
   Fridge handle (Risotto is an op-
   portunist; the fridge door wasn't
   fully closed Monday — Chef admits
   this now, red-faced — and Risotto,
   a professional, remembered the
   fridge's weakness forever).

2. The pudding rotations and the
   pyramid: Risotto walking the
   counters, nudging covers with his
   head (cats head-bump things they
   like; he likes puddings; the
   puddings were receiving affection).

3. The two missing puddings + note:
   NOT the cat. MISS AKKO HERSELF. 😱

   The full picture: Miss Akko is 88,
   retired, and — it turns out — has
   been the school's NIGHT CLEANER's
   mother-in-law, visiting from
   overseas, staying in the little
   house behind the kitchen. She
   couldn't sleep (time zones), went
   for a walk, smelled caramel
   through the vent draft, followed
   it, found the kitchen door's old
   1980s lock (which she knows how
   to open — she installed the habit
   of keeping a key under the third
   plant pot in 1975 and Gerald never
   changed it), let herself in with
   her daughter-in-law's cleaning
   key... and found puddings. Her
   puddings. Almost. Close. So close.
   She ate two (she's 88; she's
   earned it), wrote a thank-you
   note — "very good. thank you. —
   a friend" — in chocolate sauce
   because there was no pen and she
   is a professional who improvises,
   and left.

   She came back Thursday night to
   properly investigate how close
   "close" was — and found Bea's
   lopsided pudding. Tasted it.
   It was EXACTLY hers. She left
   quietly, shaken, and told her
   daughter-in-law: "someone in that
   school cooks like me. Find out
   who. And guard them."

4. Tonight at 8 pm, you two brought
   everything to Chef Bianca: the
   book, the page, the cat, the
   grandma. All of it. Chef Bianca
   held Miss Akko's recipe book like
   a holy object and didn't speak
   for a full minute. Then she said:
   "The heat. She always said my
   heat was too high." And phoned
   her. Miss Akko answered. They
   talked for two hours. There were
   tears (both sides) and technical
   caramel arguments (both sides).

5. Miss Akko now visits the kitchen
   every Thursday — officially, as
   "Quality Consultant, Emeritus."
   She and Bea cook together. She
   calls Bea "little wrist." Bea
   calls her "Chef Akko." The
   puddings have never been better.
   Chef Bianca is at peace (mostly)
   (the pudding hierarchy is a
   sensitive topic; the plaque in
   the kitchen reads "TWO CHEFS,
   ONE PUDDING, ZERO SECRETS" and
   everyone respects it).

6. Risotto was adopted. Officially.
   School cat. Title: "Senior Tuna
   Compliance Officer." The vent was
   fixed (Risotto protested; a
   proper cat door was installed in
   the back door instead — upgrade).
   The tuna ledger was balanced by
   Willa's confession and 40 hours
   of dish duty, which Willa did
   happily, humming, because the
   cat is worth it.

7. And the note? Miss Akko writes a
   chocolate-sauce note every
   Thursday now. It's on the fridge.
   This week's: "very good. getting
   better. watch the heat. — a
   friend" The friend is no longer
   a mystery. But the sauce remains.
   Some traditions are worth keeping.

WINNER: The player with more points!
EVERYONE: wins, because the ghost was
a grandma following a caramel smell,
and the monster was a cat who loves
puddings, and the real treasure was
a recipe book in a wall for 28 years.

📚 GLOSSARY 词汇表

- caramel pudding = 焦糖布丁 🍮
- inventory shrinkage = 库存损耗（Willa的替罪羊）
- quadrupedal = 四足行走的
- penmanship = 书法/笔迹
- mother-in-law = 婆婆/岳母
- emeritus = 荣誉退休的（Akko奶奶的头衔）
- identity crisis = 身份危机（Bianca主厨的布丁危机）
- hierarchy = 等级制度（布丁界的敏感话题）
- compliance officer = 合规官（Risotto的官职 🐈)
- some traditions are worth keeping = 有些传统值得保留
